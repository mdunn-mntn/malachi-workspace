2 · What was REPORTED (attribution)

  • Platform dashboards credit healthy CSF + new-subscriber volume to CTV.
  • Attribution = the industry-standard credit rule: last-touch + a 30-day view-through window. Saw a CTV ad, then converted → CTV gets the credit.
  • It is a credit-allocation metric, not a causal one — it doesn't ask whether the conversion would have happened anyway.

Attribution and incrementality are different questions — neither is "wrong." This review measures the incremental (causal) one.

3 · What ELEVENLABS found (their geo test)

  • Methods: Synthetic Control + Time-Based Regression + Diff-in-Diff; country (US vs intl) + state (48 vs GA/IL/OH go-dark).
  • Country · new subscribers: −0.0%, p=0.81 (R²=0.87, well-powered).
  • Contact Sales Forms: −104 cumulative (p=0.062); DiD −12.5% vs intl.
  • State go-dark holdout: +3.8% to +7.4% but all p>0.10, R²<0.70 (underpowered).
  • Conclusion: no statistically significant incremental lift.

Total-visit lift is window-sensitive: with a longer visit-lag window (early cohort, 7–10 days) it is +1.7%, p=0.10 — still n.s. So true total-visit lift is ~0–2%, not significant, not a flat zero. ATT (served-only vs held-out) is higher — visits +35%, conversions +32% — but that is win-selection. We formed the served-counterfactual two ways: IV/LATE TOT (ITT÷0.57) = visits ~0–3%, conversions ~−2 to −4% (both n.s.); and a ghost-win simulation (ghost bids sampled at win rate w=0.27) = +33% / +26% — barely below the raw ATT, so the bias is value-selection (we win the households we bid highest on), not frequency. Only the randomized ITT / IV-TOT remove it → ≈0.

5 · Why conversions can't be measured here (power)

  • At CVR 0.062%, detecting a 5% (relative) lift needs ~$1.83M/mo (80% power, 10% holdout).
  • At ~$1M spend the smallest detectable CVR lift is ~7% — larger than any realistic CTV effect.
  • Visits are well-powered: ~$36K detects a 5% lift (MDE ~1%).

6 · Two different questions — and why the numbers differ

Attribution (credit rule)Incrementality (causal)
QuestionDid a CTV ad precede the conversion (last-touch / 30-day view)?Would it have happened without the ad?
Visits+143% credited≈0 incremental total traffic
Conversionscredited to CTV≈0 (our ITT, their geo)

Both numbers are correct for what they measure. They differ because attribution credits served households who would have visited/converted anyway; incrementality nets that out. Two independent incrementality methods — their geo test and our household holdout — agree: no detectable incremental conversion lift.

7 · Caveats / limitations

  • Validated: delivery continuous since Feb 15 (national May 7), holdout window fully active (~1M imps/day); sample well-powered (visit MDE ~2.6%); result robust to visit-lag window.
  • Served-counterfactual (TOT) formed via IV/LATE + ghost-win simulation → both ≈0; ATT (+26–35%) is value-selection. A fully clean ghost-win needs a bid-price-conditional win model (Ryan's ghost-win-simulation service).
  • ITT is diluted by the 57% win rate — but that argues the true effect is small, not large.
  • Holdout window = 10 days (bid_price_log TTL); ghost logging live 2026-05-27, no backfill.
  • Held-out households may carry pre–May-27 ad exposure (slight downward bias on lift).
  • guid = total visits to ElevenLabs' site only (advertiser_id 51660). Their site is huge — 5.4M visitor IPs / 10d.
  • Cross-device undercount: we match CTV-impression IP (TV/home router) to web-visit IP (phone/laptop). Cross-device / cellular visits have a different IP → missed. This can bias our visit lift downward. The geo test (device-agnostic) is cleaner here and also ≈0. Fix: household-graph matching.

8 · What to do going forward

  1. Build the bid-price-conditional ghost-win model (Ryan's service) → fully clean TOT; uniform win-rate sampling leaves value-selection in.
  2. For low-CVR / B2B advertisers, measure visits, not conversions; don't sanction conversion incrementality tests below the MDE spend (~$2M for ElevenLabs).
  3. ElevenLabs specifically: conversion incrementality is unmeasurable at current spend; visit incrementality is ≈0 (clean) / unverified-positive (ATT). Set expectations accordingly.
  4. Reuse this pipeline (bid_price_log ghostBid → ITT) for the next incrementality reads.
