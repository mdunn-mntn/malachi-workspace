A campaign-grain view that classifies MNTN Matched configs, one row per active Stage 1 campaign. Join it on campaign_id, or roll up by campaign_group_id. It is not built yet; this is the proposed schema, and I want a read on the columns before materializing it. Owner: Malachi (AUDI-1083).

It grades two things, kept separate:

  • the config (mm_class) and which intent tiers it can bid (tiers_reachable)

  • how far the buyer narrowed the audience (restriction_level, plus the geo, 3P, 1P and gate flags)

They are split because two campaigns both labelled "MM" can deliver completely different tier mixes. The config decides which tiers are reachable at all. The restrictions and the gate decide how much of that actually delivers. Filtering on "has DS19" tells you neither.

The rule

MM is any of DS13, DS19 or DS46 (per the MM Taxonomy). DS19 ("MM Core") is one component inside MM, not the definition, so a "has DS19" test undercounts MM by about 7.6%.

Scoring follows the prospecting-intent model. Each IP scores on two sets: the advertiser's vertical (DS13) and the campaign's keywords (DS19). In the vertical and a keyword match scores HI (10000). In the vertical with no keyword scores PP (8000). In the bucket but not the vertical scores MI. Outside both is unscored. So HI needs both the vertical and keywords.

The vertical is the advertiser's and is always in the score, so the keyword layer (DS19) is the config lever for HI. A keyword-only config scores its keyword IPs against the advertiser's vertical and reaches HI, with no vertical-only IPs to score PP. A vertical-only config has no keywords, so its vertical IPs cap at PP and it reaches no HI. DS13 (legacy) pins at 8000, DS46 (Fangorn) spreads 6666 to 8000; neither reaches HI without keywords.

mm_class uses the names the team actually uses. Three cells carry the MM-version name: mmv2 (DS19 keywords) and mmv3 (DS19 + vertical). A DS13-only config is mmv1 if the campaign was created before the mmv3 release (~Sept 2024) and mmv3 after, so mmv3 covers two structural configs and tiers_reachable is computed from the raw DS flags, not from mm_class. The Fangorn cells keep structural names (mm_flagship_fangorn = DS19+DS46, fangorn_vertical_only = DS46-only). A campaign_created column is exposed so the mmv1/mmv3 boundary is auditable.

Restrictions

restriction_level is the most severe of geo (a sub-country geo include, or a radius), audience (a 3P segment AND-joined, or a seeded 1P list), or geo+audience. An OR-additive 3P or a CRM exclusion do not count, since neither narrows the scored pool. The gate (hhst_gated) is exposed but not folded into "unmodified", because it flips daily for pacing rather than reflecting how the campaign was built.

Scope

Active (Live) Stage 1 campaigns only. Only Stage 1 carries the datasource expression. Stage 2 and 3 retarget users already exposed at Stage 1 and hold no audience detail, so they classify through campaign_group_id against their group's Stage 1. Every Stage 2 or 3 campaign that actually delivers resolves to a Stage 1 in its group; the ones that do not are dormant, with no spend.

Decisions and open items

Settled so far: the gate is not required for "unmodified" (it flips daily for pacing); geo is a binary flag in v1, with an exact reach percent as a later add; the view refreshes daily as a SQLMesh FULL model. Still open: whether the binary geo flag is enough or it needs the exact reach percent, and any columns you would want that are not here.
