Why ITT failed — and the pivot

ITT compared everyone in buckets 100-999 against everyone in 0-99.

Only 14-16% of the treatment group was actually served. The other 84% diluted the lift to zero.

Ghost-bidding ATT compares only IPs that were served (treatment) against IPs that would have been served (biddable holdouts, proven via augmentor_log appearance).

ITT: Does being assigned matter? → flat zero.
ATT: Does actually being served matter? → answered today.

95% CI ±0.02pp. n = 22 million IPs. IVW-pooled across 7 advertisers.

Wedge inverts at peak — clickpass under-credits there by 38%.

The Northern Tool moment

The chart shows seven bars. Six tell the story we expected. One doesn't.

Northern Tool's biddable-holdout IPs visited at 5.90% in the 7-day window — without ever being served an MNTN ad.

The targeted IPs visited at 5.85%.

−0.05 percentage points the wrong way.

Yet clickpass shows Northern Tool with a +5.56pp "lift."

Every served impression followed by a visit gets attributed — regardless of whether the visit would have happened anyway.

Top of funnel: too generous. Middle of funnel: too stingy. Aggregate: errors mask each other.

Methodology

Holdout assignment. MD5('{AID}:{IP}') mod 1000 < 100. Per (advertiser, IP). Same hash as production.

prospecting_intent  → targetable IP × tier per advertiser, MAX score / week
  → split: holdouts (0-99) and targets (100-999)
augmentor_log       → 1 scan amortized across 7 advertisers
  → INNER JOIN holdouts  →  "biddable holdouts"
cost_impression_log → INNER JOIN targets  →  "served treatment"
clickpass + guid    → LEFT JOIN on (advertiser_id, IP), 3-day post-period

Cost. 18.2 TB Stage-1 smoke. 126.7 TB Stage-2 primary, 74-min wall, 560 slot-hours. Single-query batching: augmentor amortized 7×.

Caveats, honestly

  1. MAX-score collapses peak/mid into high for HexClad, First Watch, Zazzle, Northern Tool. Per-tier peak pool only includes Ferguson, Ancient Nutrition, Clayton.
  2. Biddable-holdout filter is loose. "Augmentor appearance" is the floor. Tightening (intent threshold, HHST gate) deferred to Phase 2. Treated arm has equivalent bias — comparison internally consistent.
  3. MNTN-overall-all-cells IVW pool is dominated by mid-tier low-rate cells. Leave-one-out swings it from +0.16pp to +1.33pp. We lead with per-tier numbers instead.

What's next

  • Phase 2c — iROAS. Per-advertiser incremental conversions × AOV ÷ MNTN spend. The number leadership wants.
