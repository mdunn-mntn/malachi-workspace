The real question for execs is not "what's the lift?" — it's "what should we do about Select?" This deck answers both.

What we do about it

DO Treat Select as a confirmed-incremental product. The geo + show framing genuinely drives lift, even without intent scoring on top. Brand-direct customers asking for proof can see the +2pp visit + +0.14pp conversion numbers.

DO Sell pooled, not per-advertiser, for now. No single Select advertiser hits the volume needed to power their own readout. We can promise line-of-business lift, not customer-specific lift.

CONSIDER Layer intent scoring on Select (easy-mode lift bump). If pure geo + show drives +2pp, adding the prospecting intent score on top could plausibly take it higher — the same engineering pattern PTV uses, applied to Select inventory.

UNLOCK Ghost-bidder (TI-886) makes per-advertiser readouts possible. Today we can't tell a Select customer "your lift is X" without 90+ days of data. Ghost-bidder removes the augmentor 10-day TTL ceiling and gives every customer a per-advertiser number.

Methodology — how we filtered to Select + measured lift

Select cohort: campaign_groups.product_id = 2 in coredb. 38 active advertisers in last 30d; 23 with measurable holdout/treated overlap in the 7d window. All 38 are a complete superset of the "Select Live Campaigns" advertisers list.

Holdout assignment: 10% per-(advertiser, IP) hash. Identical to TI-917 v5; product-agnostic.

Biddability filter: 99.99% of Select-served IPs appear in augmentor_log — the filter applies cleanly to Select.

Visit rate: guid_log (independent identity graph, no clickpass survivorship bias) within +3 days of impression window.

Compute path: BigQuery hit the 6-hour wall three times. Ported to Spark on Databricks (Jobs Compute) using the airflow-ti aug_log_ip feature store output. Ran in ~3 hours on 400 cores. Materialized intermediate ip_assigned to GCS to avoid 3x prospecting scans.

Caveat for execs: the per-(AID, IP) holdout doesn't restrict denominator to in-DMA. Holdout includes some out-of-DMA IPs that wouldn't have been served even without holdout. Bias is toward zero — Select's true lift is at least as large as +2.06pp.
