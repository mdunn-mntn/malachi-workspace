14-day window with refined attribution methodology

This deck is a follow-up to the original 7d Select lift deck. Two things changed:

1. Window doubled to 14 days (April 22 - May 5) for more impression volume per advertiser.

2. Attribution methodology tightened: each IP now gets a fixed 7-day attribution window from its first eligibility moment, instead of a fixed calendar visit window.

Conclusion holds: Select is genuinely incremental. The lift estimate moved slightly with methodology, not market.

The methodology fix — per-IP attribution windowing

Old (7d run): all visits within a fixed calendar window (April 29 - May 8) counted, regardless of when the IP was first served. An IP first impressed on April 29 had 10 days to convert; an IP impressed on May 5 had only 4. Visits before the impression also counted (including for holdout, where they had no impression at all).

New (14d run): each IP anchors at its first eligibility moment (first cost-impression for treated, first augmentor appearance for holdout) and gets exactly 7 days from that anchor to convert. Symmetric across treated and holdout. No pre-anchor visits counted.

Why the lift number dropped — methodology, not market

Both arms saw absolute rates fall under the new methodology — treated more than holdout. Two effects driving this:

ArmOld visit rateNew visit rateDrop
Treated 2.6334% 1.8880% -28%
Holdout 0.5782% 0.4755% -18%

1. Per-IP 7-day windows are stricter for early-impression IPs. An IP first served on day 1 of the impression window had 10 calendar days under the old method, only 7 under the new. Most IPs are first served earlier in the window, so this effect dominates.

2. 14d window pulls in earlier impressions (April 22-28). These flights had different audiences and slightly lower visit-rates than the late-window cohort.

Net: rates dropped on both arms, treated dropped more, lift narrowed. Direction unchanged: Select is incremental.

Caveat — late-window IPs are still in their attribution windows

Today is May 8. For IPs first served late in the impression window (May 3-5), the data through 5/8 only captures part of their 7-day attribution window. The lift is correspondingly understated.

Methodology details

Cohort: 23 active Select advertisers (campaign_groups.product_id = 2). Same advertisers as the 7d run plus a few more captured by the 14-day impression window.

Holdout assignment: 10% per-(advertiser, IP) hash. Identical to TI-917 v5.

Biddability filter: intersect with aug_log_ip feature-store output (pre-aggregated augmentor IPs).

Per-IP attribution (NEW in this run): each IP gets a 7-day window starting from its first eligibility date. Treated anchor = first cost_impression date. Holdout anchor = first augmentor appearance date. Visit/conversion only counts if it falls in [anchor, anchor + 6] inclusive.

Compute path: Spark on Databricks Jobs Compute (~30-35 c3d-highmem nodes), ~2 hours, ~\$100-120 cost. v3 of the lift notebook in the ticket folder.
