Ghost bidding: how MNTN measures incrementality

We measure the visits our ads cause, not the visits they touch.

1Why attribution is not enough

Attribution credits a visit to an ad whenever the ad touched it. It cannot say whether that visit would have happened anyway. Because campaigns target people already likely to convert, attributed results overstate what the ads actually changed. Incrementality answers the harder question: how many visits happened only because the ad ran? It adds to attribution rather than replacing it.

Lift = ad-group visit rate − holdout visit rateholdout visit rate
Computed per campaign from the bidder's own bid and visit logs, with a confidence interval on every estimate.

2How ghost bidding works

3Why you can trust it

  • Randomized. The holdout differs from the rest of the audience in one way only: it never sees the ad. Same standard of evidence as a clinical trial.
  • Always on, at full scale. It runs inside the bidder across prospecting campaigns, with no test setup, no geo split, and no media spent on the control group.
  • Conservative by design. Results are reported only where coverage and statistical checks pass, and visits are counted for only seven days after each ad opportunity, which understates lift rather than inflating it.

4How to read a lift number

Both groups keep visiting on their own. The served group's extra visits are the ones the ads caused: here, 12 more per 100 that would have happened anyway, so lift is +12%.
