Ghost bidding: how MNTN measures incrementality

We measure the visits our ads cause,
not the visits they touch.

Attribution credits a visit to an ad whenever the ad touched it, so it cannot say whether the visit would have happened anyway. Ghost bidding answers the harder question: how many visits happened only because the ad ran?

2Why you can trust it

  • Randomized. The holdout differs in one way only: it never sees the ad. Clinical-trial logic.
  • Always on, full scale. Runs inside the bidder on every prospecting campaign, 1,100+ advertisers, no test setup, no media spent on controls.
  • Conservative. Reported only where coverage and statistical checks pass; the short window understates lift.

3What it shows for Select

Ghost-bid measurement live since Jun 2026, prospecting campaigns. Lift is relative to each product's holdout visit rate; per-product results are statistically significant. Measured across all bid-eligible households (intent-to-treat). Full method and per-advertiser detail: AUDI-1172 workbook.
