Keywords are important.

Keyword Importance Is Advertiser-Specific

Some keywords are inherently valuable across the board — regardless of advertiser or vertical, certain keywords produce ~3x lift globally.

But when we rank keywords per advertiser, lift jumps to 72x.

The value isn't just in having keywords — it's in knowing which keywords matter most for each advertiser. That's the signal continuous scoring captures.

So What?

1. Keyword ranking carries a massive signal.

72x aggregate lift, 82x median per-advertiser. The difference between top-ranked and bottom-ranked keywords is not incremental — it's an order of magnitude.

2. We're not using this signal today.

Every keyword-matched IP gets a flat 10,000 score. Rank 1 and rank 200 are treated identically. Continuous scoring would let us focus spend on the highest-signal IPs first, then expand with pacing.

Next Steps

Validate with existing experiments

Score IPs with keyword rankings on experiments already run. Do higher-ranked IPs show better impression → visit rates in treatment groups?

Implement continuous scoring

Use keyword rankings to score IPs within high intent (8,000–10,000) instead of a flat 10,000. Gives pacing finer-grained control to focus budget on highest-signal IPs first.

Anticipated Questions

QuestionAnswer
"72x seems high — is this a fair comparison?"We're comparing the best keywords to the worst. But today, all of them are scored 10,000 — treated identically. The comparison shows what we're leaving on the table by not ranking.
"Visit rates are tiny"Denominator is 30.9B IPs. Absolute rate reflects the enormous pool. 72x relative lift is what matters — it tells the pacing system which IPs to prioritize.
"Driven by a few big advertisers?"106 of 125 show >10x lift independently. Median 82x across 67 verticals. Not outlier-driven.
"Is this causal?"Observational with temporal separation. Monotonic across 6 rank buckets, 125 advertisers, 67 verticals. Validation against existing experiments is the next step.
"How would this change bidding?"Today: all keyword IPs = 10,000. With ranking: top-ranked IPs = 10,000, lower-ranked scale down to 8,000. Pacing team gets finer control. Audience can be larger without sacrificing signal.
"What about sizing issues from past experiments?"Past experiments treated all BUK keywords uniformly (audience too large → performance declined). Continuous scoring solves this: larger audience with ranked scoring focuses budget on highest-signal IPs first.
