So What?

1. Keyword selection is the highest-leverage targeting lever we have.

72x aggregate, 82x median per-advertiser. Confirmed across 500 advertisers and 67 verticals.

2. The value is advertiser-specific.

Global ranking gives 3x. Per-advertiser gives 72x aggregate. Only a model that learns from cross-advertiser behavioral data can capture this.

3. We're currently throwing this signal away.

Every BUK-matched IP gets a flat 10,000 score regardless of rank. We treat a 72x signal as binary.

Anticipated Questions

QuestionAnswer
"Isn't this circular?"Keywords 3/1–3/15, visits 3/16–3/26. Temporal separation by design. ALS model trained on history (standard), evaluation window is clean.
"Visit rates are tiny"Denominator is 30.9B IPs. Absolute rate reflects enormous denominator, not weak signal. 72x relative rate is what matters for targeting.
"Driven by a few big advertisers?"106/125 show >10x lift independently. Median 82x. Signal is consistent across 67 verticals.
"Why did the lift drop from 184x to 72x?"Expected dilution — more advertisers in the pool. Per-advertiser median (82x) is the meaningful metric. 85% >10x confirms robustness.
"Is this causal?"Observational with strong design. Monotonic across 6 buckets, 67 verticals, temporal separation. RCT is TI-806.
"What about IP rotation?"Adds noise biasing toward zero. 72x is a lower bound on true signal.
"Comparison to current system?"Flat 10,000 score for all BUK IPs. We treat a 72x signal as binary.
