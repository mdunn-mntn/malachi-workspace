WHAT WE SHOULD PAY FOR 5x5 -- THE LOGIC

Every number is built from two facts:
1) The market rate we already pay comparable site-visit vendors is $0.50 per 1,000
   impressions (CPM = cost per thousand; confirmed in our own data -- data costs
   ~$0.001/impression, i.e. ~$1 per 1,000).
2) 5x5's data touches ~34.35M impressions/day (~12.5B/yr); ~213.5K/day go to
   households ONLY 5x5 saw.

Apply the rate to those impression counts:

- Walk-away ceiling ~ $525K/mo ($6.3M/yr): 34.35M/day x $0.50/1,000 x 365.
  What 5x5 would cost at the peer rate on EVERY impression it touches -- a hard
  upper bound, since 74% of its IPs we already see (most of that traffic happens
  with or without it). Above this we are provably overpaying.

- Floor ~ $3K/mo ($40K/yr): 213.5K/day x $0.50/1,000 x 365.
  The undeniable value -- impressions to households ONLY 5x5 brought. A floor
  because it ignores 5x5's bigger value (unique domains that sharpen targeting for
  households we also see elsewhere).

- Fair ~ $15-50K/mo (~$180-600K/yr); anchor ~$25-30K/mo: the band between floor
  and ceiling. Triangulated two ways: (a) 5x5 supplies ~12% of MNTN Matched's
  usable domain signal, and MM touches ~$210-385M/yr of media, so a single-digit-%
  slice lands in the low-to-mid six figures; (b) a typical good data-partner flat
  fee is tens-to-low-hundreds of $K/yr, and 5x5 is our #2 most-unique partner, so it
  sits at the upper end. The band is pinned by the two hard anchors; the exact point
  is judgment.

- Volume minimums (>= 2.5B rows/mo AND >= 25M unique IPxdomain pairs/day): a flat
  fee is only fair at the volume we measured. The row floor sits just below current
  delivery (~2.8B/mo). The unique-pair floor stops padding with duplicate/junk rows
  -- it protects the real value unit (distinct site-visits), since 5x5's 93M daily
  events collapse to only ~33M distinct pairs.

- If billed as CPM instead: the rate depends entirely on the base. On MATCHED
  impressions, $0.50 is fair (peer parity). On ALL touched impressions, only
  $0.02-0.05 is fair (~95% is redundant) -- $0.50 on all-touched is the $6.3M trap.
  So demand CPM be billed on matched impressions, not all touched.

BOTTOM LINE: anchor on ~$25-30K/mo with the two volume minimums; renew under
~$50K/mo, renegotiate above, walk only near ~$525K/mo. (Floor and ceiling are
measured; the fair point is an estimate -- a precise number would need an
add/remove model test.)
