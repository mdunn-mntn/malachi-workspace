## 3. Buildable now

**3a. IMP-132 — audit every detector against the meters. Highest leverage item on this list.**
`ipdsc_ds_49` cut disk spill 94% and cost 2.5x more. Dataproc bills machine-time, and shuffle storage
is provisioned **per machine**, so **spill is structurally unbilled** — a spill fix recovers nothing on
any invoice line by construction. `disk_spill` (42) and `shuffle_fetch_wait` (70) are **two-thirds of
the 175 open findings**, so a large share of the backlog may be ranked by a quantity nobody pays for.
Every detector needs to say which meter it moves and in which direction, or say that it is unknown.
