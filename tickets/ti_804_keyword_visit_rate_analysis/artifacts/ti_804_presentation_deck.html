So What?

1. Keyword selection is the highest-leverage targeting lever we have.

184x differential — picking the right 5 keywords is worth 184x more than the bottom of the list.

2. The value is advertiser-specific.

Global ranking gives 3x. Per-advertiser gives 184x. Only a model that learns from cross-advertiser behavioral data can capture this.

3. We're currently throwing this signal away.

Every BUK-matched IP gets a flat 10,000 score regardless of rank. We treat a 184x signal as binary.

Anticipated Questions

QuestionAnswer
"Isn't this circular?"Keywords 3/1–3/15, visits 3/16–3/26. Temporal separation by design. ALS model trained on history (standard), evaluation window is clean.
"Visit rates are tiny"Denominator is 3B IPs. Absolute rate reflects enormous denominator, not weak signal. 184x relative rate is what matters for targeting.
"Driven by a few big advertisers?"14/15 show >10x lift independently. Excluding top 3, median remains triple digits.
"Only 15 of 50 had enough data">10 filter is for statistical stability. Small advertisers can't produce reliable ratios in 10 days. TI-808 scales to 500.
"Is this causal?"Observational with strong design. Monotonic across 6 buckets, 15 verticals, temporal separation. RCT is TI-806.
"Why 50 advertisers?"Phase 1 scope. Deterministic hash, not cherry-picked. 93% consistency at n=50 makes reversal implausible.
"What about IP rotation?"Adds noise biasing toward zero. 184x is a lower bound on true signal.
"Comparison to current system?"Flat 10,000 score for all BUK IPs. We treat a 184x signal as binary.
