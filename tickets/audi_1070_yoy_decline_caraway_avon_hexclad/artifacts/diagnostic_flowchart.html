Prospecting ROAS/Visits Declined YoY — Diagnostic Decision Tree

Walk 0→5: fix the lens & scope, rule out tracking outages, then the delivery spine. “MM degraded” is the verdict only if every kill-condition fails.

Malachi Dunn · AUDI-1070 · reusable for any advertiser × any two periods

artifact Artifact — re-pull / re-baseline, no delivery fix
healthy Healthy — no action; the 'decline' is a misperception
real-decline Real decline — a concrete fix applies
needs-more Needs more — gather evidence / re-open

Decision nodes — what to ask, and the exact table to check

The spine runs cheapest-artifact-first. Q2 is the fork between the two failure modes (delivery left High-Intent via a gate flip, vs delivery stayed in HI but saturated).

NodeQuestion & branchesHow to check (table / column)
Q0
Same lens + same scope + same denominator, both years?
Normalize BEFORE measuring anything. (1) LENS: re-pull BOTH years under ONE fixed reporting_style and ONE lookback. The 2025-12-10 bulk last_touch->industry_standard migration alone manufactures a large apparent drop (Avon/HexClad UI genuinely compared 2025-LT vs 2026-FT -> ~52-54pp of the apparent crash is the switch). Client UI (CHAPI->ClickHouse, industry_standard = last-touch + competing_* credit) is ~5x visits / ~2-3x ROAS vs a naive last-touch BQ pull; conversions are last-touch/last-tv-touch only (no first-touch conv table) -> the uplift is the competing_* columns. reporting_style OSCILLATES historically (dozens of flips) -> resolve the EFFECTIVE value as-of each window from the archive, do not trust the single last-modified date. (2) SCOPE: same funnel stage AND same channel (Stage-1 prospecting is CTV-only; S2/S3 add display) — a channel-mix shift is apples-to-oranges. (3) DENOMINATOR: same 10% holdout treatment (MD5(AID:IP) mod 1000, 0-99 = holdout, per-advertiser per-IP) both years — use ITT consistently. KILL: if the decline shrinks/vanishes once lens+scope+denominator are held constant, or it is a wrong YoY window (2024->2025 scale-up), it is a measurement artifact.
shrinks / vanishes on fixed lens+scope; or wrong window; or channel/holdout mismatch→MEASUREMENT-LENS / WINDOW / SCOPE ARTIFACT
persists on one fixed, correctly-scoped lens→Discrete visit/conversion TRACKING OUTAGE in either window?
 Lens+migration+oscillation history: bronze.integrationprod.archives_advertiser_setting_archives (resolve effective reporting_style as-of each window; single last-modified date on r2_advertiser_settings is unreliable). Lookback: silver.audience.advertiser_configurations. Channel/stage scope: bronze.integrationprod.campaigns.channel_id (8=CTV,1=display), campaigns.funnel_level + objective_id IN (1,5,6); impression partner_ad_format VIDEO/BANNER. Holdout: MD5('{AID}:{IP}') mod 1000 in {0..99}. Reproduce UI as last-touch + competing_* (silver.summarydata.all_facts) vs sum_by_advertiser_by_day on ONE lens.
Q0b
Discrete visit/conversion TRACKING OUTAGE in either window?
Cheapest data-quality artifact, checked BEFORE the waterfall because an unexcluded crater corrupts every downstream factor (drags VisitRate at Q1, distorts served-score reads at Q2-Q4). SIGNATURE: one metric (views OR raw conversion-pixel fires) craters to ~0 for a BOUNDED date range while impressions/spend stay NORMAL and the OTHER metric keeps tracking; synchronized across ALL campaigns incl. retargeting; discrete start + instant snap-back. This is a pipeline/pixel break, NOT audience quality, and is DISTINCT from the attribution-lens artifact at Q0. HexClad Mar 3-17 2026 VV gap (~3,000->20 views/day, conversions FLAT, snap-back Mar 18) was 'the steepest drop in the entire series' and inflates HexClad's reported -38% VR by ~6-13pp. Caraway ~Jan 6 2026 conversion-pixel break (raw fires ~2,500->800/day at constant impressions) is a large slice of its Q1 ROAS cliff — NOT pure saturation.
discrete crater, one metric ->0, other holds, snap-back→TRACKING / PIXEL OUTAGE (route to Pixel Ops)
no outage (or exclude the window) -> localize→Waterfall: which factor drives the YoY change? (within-stratum vs mix; new-campaign ramp)
 silver.summarydata.sum_by_campaign_by_day daily: scan for craters where views->~0 (or conversion_log fires ->~0) while impressions/spend hold and the other metric holds; confirm synchronized across all campaigns incl. retargeting + instant snap-back. Raw pixel: silver.logdata.conversion_log daily fire count. Confirm NOT platform-wide (compare cohort VR same days). Verify no HHST/config/launch/migration event aligns with the crater dates.
Q1
Waterfall: which factor drives the YoY change? (within-stratum vs mix; new-campaign ramp)
Decompose ROAS = (1000/CPM) * VisitRate * ConvRate * AOV (log-decomposition to residual ~0) and attribute the YoY delta. Compute all rate metrics with any Q0b outage window EXCLUDED. Also run within-stratum vs between-stratum (Oaxaca, campaign grain): (a) CPM-dominated with rates holding = repricing (healthy). (b) AOV-dominated with conversion COUNT holding = macro/promo/basket. (c) a SEPARATE campaign at HHST=0 / 100%-unscored dragging the blended aggregate = portfolio/mix. (d) a BRAND-NEW mega-prospecting campaign still inside its first ~4-week ramp (TI-780) that under-performs by construction = cold-start (HexClad 0->38.3M / Caraway 1.8M->26.4M new-campaign impressions). Only a within-flagship VR/ConvRate collapse at FLAT AOV (a conversion-COUNT / audience-quality problem) proceeds to the delivery spine.
CPM up, rates flat/up, spend down (repricing)→HEALTHY / REPRICING (CPM up, rates hold)
AOV/basket-driven, conv COUNT holds→MACRO / PROMO / BASKET / COHORT-WIDE ROAS
cohort-wide ROAS drop, hits flat-spend peers too→MACRO / PROMO / BASKET / COHORT-WIDE ROAS
2nd 0-HHST/100%-unscored campaign drags rollup→PORTFOLIO / MIX (2nd 0-HHST campaign drags rollup)
new campaign inside first ~4-wk ramp (TI-780)→NEW-CAMPAIGN COLD-START RAMP (TI-780)
within-flagship VR/ConvRate collapse, AOV flat→Did delivery LEAVE High-Intent? — via a GATE FLIP? (THE SPINE)
 silver.summarydata.sum_by_campaign_by_day at campaign grain (back to 2024) for per-factor CPM/VR/ConvRate/AOV, per-campaign mix (Oaxaca within/between), and campaign census/lifespan (launch date -> flag campaigns <4wk old = TI-780 ramp). AOV via ui_conversions.order_amt (order_amt_usd is NULL). Flag campaigns at HHST=0 via silver.archives.household_score_threshold_archives; flagship vs secondary in bronze.integrationprod.campaigns / campaign_groups.
Q2
Did delivery LEAVE High-Intent? — via a GATE FLIP? (THE SPINE)
THE fork between the two failure modes. Test the GATE-VALUE archive, not just served composition: did the HHST archive show a flip to 0/-1 AND does served HI-share invert the day AFTER the flip (corr~1 => gate-driven)? DISAMBIGUATOR (critical): a falling scored-fraction WITHOUT a gate flip is over-spend pushing delivery DOWN the score curve (saturation), NOT gate-removal — Caraway's AHS-scored share fell as spend scaled while its flagship gate held at 10000, so served composition alone would mis-route it here. RTC HANDLING: first TEST whether RTC is present at all (model_params realtime_conquest_score > 0). For most advertisers RTC is ABSENT — realtime_conquest_score is logged as -1 on ~100% of rows regardless — so the exclusion is a NO-OP; do NOT treat a clean HI-share as evidence you filtered RTC. Only exclude WHERE realtime_conquest_score=10000 when RTC is actually present. DATA GUARD: served-score history begins 2025-05-06 (advertiser_household_score NULL before ~Jun 2025); a pre-May-2025 baseline year has NO served-score series -> substitute HHST-archive + reach/frequency + scored-fraction-vs-spend as the HI-leave evidence.
HHST flipped to 0/-1, HI-share inverts day-after→Gate left HI — restored, or still off (holiday-drop / short-flight auto-0)?
no gate flip; stayed in HI (scored-frac drop is spend-driven)→Stayed in HI — is the score drop a SPEND-INVARIANT relabel, or spend-locked?
 Gate flip: silver.archives.household_score_threshold_archives (live: silver.dso.household_score_thresholds). Value map: 10000=HI-only, 6666=HI+Peak, 8000=Peak, 3333/3334=Mid, 0/-1=no gate. Served composition (from 2025-05-06 fwd, RTC excluded ONLY if present): silver.logdata.cost_impression_log advertiser_household_score. Confirm delivery inverts day-after-flip (corr~1). If baseline predates 2025-05-06, use HHST archive + reach/freq instead of a served-score YoY.
Q2a
Gate left HI — restored, or still off (holiday-drop / short-flight auto-0)?
Delivery left HI via HHST=0. Is the gate now RESTORED (holiday drop that was re-gated, HI-share recovered) or STILL OFF? If still off: a one-off holiday HHST=0 never reverted AND/OR continuously re-forced by SHORT FLIGHTS (any flight <72h auto-sets HHST=0 — a client campaign-management / PEX deliverability practice), often with a second campaign running 100% unscored. Restored => self-corrected transient (recovery leaf); still-off => GATE-REMOVAL.
re-gated on holiday, HI-share recovered→HEALTHY-RECOVERY — gate dropped on holiday, RE-GATED
gate=0 never reverted / short-flight re-forced→GATE REMOVAL — delivery left High-Intent (HHST=0)
 silver.archives.household_score_threshold_archives for the drop date AND any restore event; silver.core.flights (start_time, end_time) — runs <72h auto-force HHST=0; bronze.integrationprod.campaigns for a parallel 100%-unscored campaign. Confirm HI-share recovery (or not) in silver.logdata.cost_impression_log.
Q3
Stayed in HI — is the score drop a SPEND-INVARIANT relabel, or spend-locked?
Delivery held in HI but performance fell. KILL-CONDITION before any saturation/degradation claim: is the apparent quality drop a spend-INVARIANT composition/relabel (a %unscored / Max-Reach (1-3332, random by construction) mix shift, or a DS13->DS46 Fangorn engine swap / PP-launch / Max-Reach-off regime relabel) with a STABLE within-HI median and an intact score->VR gradient? SPEND-CONTROL TEST (decisive): is the rising %unscored / below-8000 correlated with THIS advertiser's own monthly spend (spend-locked, recedes when spend drops, e.g. HexClad corr +0.76)? If YES it is the over-scaling MECHANISM -> go to Q4, NOT a relabel. Only a spend-INVARIANT shift tied to a platform regime DATE (e.g. Avon's AHS-under-8000 stayed 3-9% at flat spend) is the benign artifact. (household_score is binary ~all 10000 -> reason on within-HI median + gradient, never the mean.)
spend-INVARIANT relabel; within-HI median stable, gradient intact→SCORE-SIDE / REGIME-RELABEL ARTIFACT (spend-invariant mix)
spend-locked scored-frac drop, or real within-HI VR collapse remains→Saturation / EXPANSION signature at held HI-share?
 silver.logdata.cost_impression_log: %unscored / Max-Reach / continuous-Fangorn (8001-9999) mix, within-HI median, score->VR gradient, AND corr(%under-8000, monthly spend) for THIS advertiser. Regime dates: silver.archives.audience_segment_archives / bronze.integrationprod.archives_audience_segment_archives (confirm HI substrate DS13 ∩ DS19 stayed put).
Q4
Saturation / EXPANSION signature at held HI-share?
At held HI-share, did the flagship over-scale a FINITE HI pool? PRIMARY signature is EXPANSION, not frequency saturation: REACH (unique users) grows while within-HI visits/user (VR) collapses, at FLAT-OR-FALLING frequency (HexClad freq 2.90->2.86 flat, Caraway 2.99->2.72 DOWN; reach +19% / +127%). Do NOT require frequency to rise — the mechanism is 'genuinely more, lower-quality users', not 'same users more often'. Confirming signals: within-HI VR collapse at constant HI-share (Caraway Mar26 99.9% HI @ 0.15% VR vs Jul25 99% HI @ 0.37% VR, x0.53 within the gated 10000 band), cumulative distinct HI reached crossing the live pool, brand-new-HI share falling (100%->35%), spend ramp (+191%). household_score is BINARY (~all 10000) so this is INVISIBLE to any score/avg-score dashboard — you MUST measure VR-WITHIN-HI, reach, and new-HI-share, never mean score.
reach expanded into lower-intent, within-HI VR collapsed at held HI-share→OVER-SCALING / SATURATION — exhausted the finite HI pool
no expansion signature; pool NOT exhausted, gate stable→COHORT PLACEMENT — the falsification backbone (spend-growth-matched)
 silver.logdata.cost_impression_log filtered to advertiser_household_score=10000 (RTC excluded only if present): within-HI VR by month, cumulative distinct HI IPs vs live 30-day HI-pool inflow, new-HI share, reach (HLL uniques) growth, impressions-per-IP frequency. Confirm spend ramp (+191% type) in silver.summarydata.sum_by_campaign_by_day.
Q5
COHORT PLACEMENT — the falsification backbone (spend-growth-matched)
FINAL KILL-CONDITION for 'general MM degradation': (a) is THIS advertiser worse than SPEND-GROWTH-MATCHED peers (not the raw cohort median — the saturation baseline must be controlled) on within-HI VR delta, AND (b) does MM-share predict decline across the cohort, AND (c) did the within-HI score->VR gradient actually break? SEPARATE the components: spend-driven VR saturation is spend-specific; a cohort-wide ROAS drop that also hits FLAT-SPEND peers (x0.57 even at flat spend) is macro/attribution, NOT MM — it must NOT satisfy the 'worse than cohort' condition on ROAS. Note a scaler can be worse than growth-peers due to HOW it scaled (concentrated mega-prospecting, TI-780 ramp) — that excess is advertiser-specific, still NOT systemic MM. Only if all three hold on a spend-growth-matched, macro-decomposed basis is genuine degradation supported.
worse than growth-matched peers AND MM-share predicts decline AND gradient broke→GENUINE MM DEGRADATION (every kill-condition failed)
not worse than growth-peers / MM-share doesn't predict / gradient intact→IDIOSYNCRATIC / NEEDS MORE — 'MM degradation' UNSUPPORTED
 Run the parameterized diagnostic across a peer cohort (same vertical / matched gate / matched spend-growth decile). Rank this advertiser's within-HI VR delta vs spend-growth-MATCHED peers (not raw median) and regress cohort decline on MM-share, holding the flat-spend cohort-wide ROAS component out: silver.summarydata.sum_by_campaign_by_day + silver.logdata.cost_impression_log. Effective-audience changes: bronze.integrationprod.archives_audience_segment_archives.

Diagnosis leaves — verdict, exemplar, fix

Sorted by severity. The four real-decline leaves are the ones that carry a concrete fix.

NodeDiagnosis & fixConfirm with
D_gate_removal
GATE REMOVAL — delivery left High-Intent (HHST=0) real-decline gate-removal
exemplar: HexClad (34611)
Fix / action: Restore & HOLD the gate at 10000 (or its prior 6666 HI+Peak); run flights >=4 days (never <72h, which auto-forces HHST=0); pace ~$5K/day so a scale-up doesn't drain HI. Kill or gate the second 100%-unscored campaign. Root cause = a Nov-11 holiday drop to 0 never reverted + re-forced by short-flight budgeting — a client campaign-management (PEX) behavior, not the MM model. Conversions HALVED at flat AOV confirms audience-quality (count), not basket. (Note: HexClad ALSO has a Mar 3-17 VV tracking outage — rule that out at Q0b first; the gate is the standing decline.)
 silver.archives.household_score_threshold_archives (never-reverted 0) + silver.core.flights (<72h runs) + silver.logdata.cost_impression_log (HI-share 98%->31%)
D_macro
MACRO / PROMO / BASKET / COHORT-WIDE ROAS real-decline
Fix / action: Not an audience-quality problem. Two sub-cases: (1) AOV/basket-driven with conversion COUNT holding -> promo cadence, price/discount, seasonality, product mix; route to merchandising. (2) A cohort-wide ROAS decline that hits even FLAT-SPEND peers (x0.57 across all deciles) -> market/macro/seasonal (and possibly the LT->FT-migration) effect on everyone, distinct from the spend-driven VR/saturation component. Neither is MM-specific and neither may satisfy the D_mm_degraded 'worse than cohort' ROAS condition. Contrast with the gate/over-scaling leaves where AOV is FLAT and it is conversion COUNT that fell.
 silver.summarydata.sum_by_campaign_by_day (AOV via ui_conversions.order_amt; confirm conv count flat AND/OR ROAS falls even for flat-spend cohort peers)
D_mm_degraded
GENUINE MM DEGRADATION (every kill-condition failed) real-decline
Fix / action: Escalate to the audience/scoring team (Zach Schoenberger for audience-platform/expression): the MM model's within-HI discrimination genuinely weakened for this advertiser AND across the cohort (worse than SPEND-GROWTH-MATCHED peers AND MM-share predicts decline AND the score->VR gradient broke, with the macro/attribution component decomposed out). The ONLY leaf where 'the audience degraded' is supported — every measurement, outage, mix, cold-start, gate, recovery, score-artifact, and saturation kill-condition has already failed. Investigate the DS13 ∩ DS19 substrate / scoring pipeline. File an audience-model investigation ticket; do not ship a client-facing claim until the cohort evidence holds. (HexClad's within-prospecting 0.419%->0.145% at constant volume is a real candidate signal, but it survives here ONLY if it beats the spend-growth-matched baseline.)
 silver.summarydata.sum_by_campaign_by_day + silver.logdata.cost_impression_log (cohort within-HI VR vs spend-growth-matched peers + MM-share regression + broken within-HI gradient)
D_over_scaling
OVER-SCALING / SATURATION — exhausted the finite HI pool real-decline over-scaling
exemplar: Caraway (40341)
Fix / action: Pace HI spend to the pool's inflow (replacement) rate, not to a budget target; widen the addressable pool (add keywords -> more DS19 breadth); adopt continuous scoring (Fangorn) to grade WITHIN HI so the collapse becomes visible. Mechanism: nearly tripled spend (+191%) into a finite HI pool at held HI-share, reached MORE unique users (+127%) at flat/falling frequency (EXPANSION, not 'same users more often'), exhausted responsive HI and reached weaker HI -> within-HI VR collapsed x0.53 at constant HI-share (even inside the gated 10000 band); new-HI share fell 100%->35%. household_score is binary (~all 10000) so this is INVISIBLE to score dashboards — measure VR-within-HI + reach + new-HI-share. NOTE: a scaler can be worse than growth-matched peers due to concentrated mega-prospecting (HOW it scaled) — still advertiser-specific, NOT systemic MM. Caraway's Q1 ROAS cliff also has a Jan conversion-pixel-break slice (rule out at Q0b).
 silver.logdata.cost_impression_log (advertiser_household_score=10000: within-HI VR, reach, cumulative distinct HI vs live pool, new-HI share, frequency) + silver.summarydata.sum_by_campaign_by_day (spend +191%)
D_measurement
MEASUREMENT-LENS / WINDOW / SCOPE ARTIFACT artifact
exemplar: Avon (31921)
Fix / action: Hold everything constant across BOTH years: same reporting_style (don't straddle the 2025-12-10 industry_standard migration; resolve the EFFECTIVE value from the oscillating archive, not the last-modified date), same lookback, same funnel-stage + channel (Stage-1 CTV-only vs S2/S3 display), same 10% holdout (ITT). Reproduce client UI as last-touch + competing_* before comparing to any naive BQ pull. Use the correct YoY window (not a 2024->2025 scale-up). The lens switch alone manufactured ~52-54pp of Avon/HexClad's apparent crash. Re-state on the fixed lens; deliver the reconciled number. NOTE: if the fixed-lens numbers come back HEALTHY (rates up), the primary diagnosis is D_healthy — this leaf only explains the client's misperception. No delivery change — analyst re-pull + CS re-message.
 bronze.integrationprod.archives_advertiser_setting_archives (effective reporting_style + oscillation history) + silver.audience.advertiser_configurations (lookback) + silver.summarydata.all_facts (industry_standard reproduction)
D_portfolio
PORTFOLIO / MIX (2nd 0-HHST campaign drags rollup) artifact
Fix / action: Decompose the prospecting rollup by campaign — a second campaign at 0/-1 HHST (100% unscored) or a DMA/test campaign (Caraway ran 2 ungated DMA tests; HexClad ran a parallel 100%-unscored campaign) lowers the blended AGGREGATE with no per-campaign degradation (between-stratum mix). The API aggregates all prospecting together. Report the flagship separately; gate or pause the diluting campaign if it isn't a deliberate test. Not model degradation.
 silver.summarydata.sum_by_campaign_by_day (per-campaign split) x silver.archives.household_score_threshold_archives (which campaigns sit at 0)
D_score_artifact
SCORE-SIDE / REGIME-RELABEL ARTIFACT (spend-invariant mix) artifact
Fix / action: No audience fix — a spend-INVARIANT composition/relabel: rising %unscored or Max-Reach mix (Max Reach 1-3332 random by construction), or a DS13->DS46 (Fangorn) engine swap / PP-launch / Max-Reach-off regime relabel, tied to a platform DATE and stable at flat spend (e.g. Avon AHS-under-8000 3-9% at flat spend). Within-HI median and the score->VR gradient are intact (MM still discriminates). Re-baseline the score bands to the new regime and compare like-for-like. CAUTION: if the %unscored rise is SPEND-LOCKED for this advertiser (recedes when spend drops), it is NOT a relabel — it is the over-scaling mechanism (Q4/D_over_scaling). Report 'general MM degradation' as UNSUPPORTED.
 silver.logdata.cost_impression_log (spend-INVARIANT %unscored, stable within-HI median, intact gradient) + silver.archives.audience_segment_archives (regime / DS46 / Max-Reach / PP dates)
D_tracking_outage
TRACKING / PIXEL OUTAGE (route to Pixel Ops) artifact
exemplar: HexClad Mar 3-17 VV gap / Caraway Jan conv-pixel break
Fix / action: Discrete data-quality break, not audience quality. Exclude the outage window(s) and re-state the YoY. Route to Pixel Ops (Ashley Pineda Varela) — these gaps also affect the client UI. HexClad's Mar 3-17 VV gap alone inflated the reported -38% VR by ~6-13pp; Caraway's ~Jan-6 conversion-pixel break is a large slice of its Q1 ROAS cliff wrongly attributed to saturation. Signature to cite: one metric ->~0 for a bounded range at constant impressions/spend, other metric holds, synchronized across ALL campaigns incl. retargeting, instant snap-back, advertiser-specific (not platform-wide).
 silver.summarydata.sum_by_campaign_by_day (daily views vs impressions vs conversions craters) + silver.logdata.conversion_log (raw pixel fire count)
D_healthy
HEALTHY / REPRICING (CPM up, rates hold) healthy
exemplar: Avon (31921)
Fix / action: No fix — not a decline. Primary Avon leaf. CPM rose (paid more for better inventory) so impressions/reach fell (contraction), but every rate metric held or improved: ROAS +8-19%, spend -14-18%, conv-rate +22%, visits/user +17%, AOV flat. Its within-HI prospecting VR actually ROSE (x2.2) when spend wasn't pushed — the clean control that falsifies 'general MM degradation'. MoM VR wobble is low-volume noise (200-800K imps/month). CS reframes 'fewer, better users' to the client.
 silver.summarydata.sum_by_campaign_by_day (CPM up, VR/CR/ROAS up, AOV flat) + silver.logdata.cost_impression_log (within-HI VR rose at flat spend)
D_recovery
HEALTHY-RECOVERY — gate dropped on holiday, RE-GATED healthy
exemplar: Avon (31921)
Fix / action: No standing fix — a transient, self-corrected dip. The gate was removed over a holiday (Avon: Nov-19, up to 61% of Dec impressions unscored) but RE-GATED (Jan 6) and HI-share recovered (to 99.9% HI by Feb). Any December dip is a bounded transient, not a trend. Owner action: confirm the re-gate held; if the holiday-off recurs, add a guardrail to auto-restore the gate after the promo window.
 silver.archives.household_score_threshold_archives (drop event + restore date) + silver.logdata.cost_impression_log (HI-share recovery)
D_cold_start
NEW-CAMPAIGN COLD-START RAMP (TI-780) needs-more
Fix / action: The YoY delta is partly a brand-new mega-prospecting campaign still inside its first ~4-week learning ramp (TI-780), which under-performs by construction (HexClad 0->38.3M / Caraway 1.8M->26.4M new-campaign impressions). Exclude ramp weeks (or require n_post >= 28 days) before concluding audience quality; re-evaluate once the new campaign matures. If the under-performance persists past the ramp AND the campaign is a scaled prospecting flagship, re-route to the saturation branch (Q4). Not degradation, not a permanent verdict.
 silver.summarydata.sum_by_campaign_by_day (campaign launch date + first-4-week VR ramp) + bronze.integrationprod.campaigns (campaign age)
D_needs_more
IDIOSYNCRATIC / NEEDS MORE — 'MM degradation' UNSUPPORTED needs-more
Fix / action: Cohort placement falsified general MM degradation: not worse than spend-growth-matched peers, or MM-share doesn't predict decline, or the within-HI gradient is intact. Do NOT conclude model degradation. Re-open for advertiser-specific levers — creative fatigue, landing-page/checkout change, competitive/seasonal shift, or an effective-audience change (3P intersection, geo narrowing, CRM include/exclude, op:not excludes) — or gather more post-period volume (n_post >= 28 days). (Pixel/tracking gaps and new-campaign cold-start now have their own earlier leaves — D_tracking_outage, D_cold_start — so this leaf is the genuinely idiosyncratic residual only.)
 silver.summarydata.sum_by_campaign_by_day (spend-growth-matched placement + MM-share regression) + bronze.integrationprod.archives_audience_segment_archives (effective-audience changes)
