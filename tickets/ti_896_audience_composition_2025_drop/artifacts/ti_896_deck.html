Caveats

  • DS2 is OPM segments, NOT Mountain Matched (per Alyson + Zach 2026-04-22). Bryce's earlier "MM = DS2" mapping was wrong. Mountain Matched the product (MM 2.0) is not detectable by current methodology — needs definition from Ryan / Zach.
  • "PP-enabled" detector (DS13+DS19+rtc) catches audiences enabling Mid Intent + High Intent + PP tiers within MM 2.0 — not specifically PP-tier-only.
  • 24% of PP-detector expressions also contain DS2; buckets partially overlap.
  • New PP-enabled cohort had ~1.5x higher baseline ROAS than non-adopters; cohorts not exchangeable, lift comparison doesn't isolate PP's effect.
  • 3P scope is narrow (DS35 only); DS3 / DS11 / DS17 / DS18 etc. excluded.
