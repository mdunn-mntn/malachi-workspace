Three takeaways

1. Peak Performance reached 12% of currently-active advertisers (and 12% of cohort spend). Near-zero pre-launch, inflection at Oct 6.

2. Mountain Matched spend collapsed from 73-79% to 42-46% in a single week (Oct 27 2025). This is the larger spend movement; it is co-equal with PP in importance.

3. Other buckets moved modestly (-2 to -5pp). The original "everything else flat" framing was an artifact of paused-campaign attribution.

Track B — Default vs custom PP

Two ways to run Peak Performance:

Default — audience template carries the minimal PP pattern (DS13 intent + DS19 keywords) and nothing else.

Custom — advertiser layers on additional DS clauses: exclusion lists, CRM overlays, extra keyword groups, geo logic.

Random-sample discovery (Fix M7) confirmed structural split: 39% pure DS13+DS19, 48% layered, 14% heavily-layered.

Classifier limits: structural proxy at template level; doesn't capture campaign-level layering. Formal definition of "default" is open for audience-tools team.

~61% of adopters run custom PP; ~32% use the default. Split has been stable across the entire ramp — no drift over time. Majority are modifying the recommended template.

Adopters started with ~1.5x higher baseline ROAS (Aug-Sep ~28-31 vs ~17-25 for non-adopters). The two cohorts are not exchangeable — adopters self-selected. Both lifted Q4. Attributing the gap to PP requires propensity matching; this analysis does not do that.

Within new adopters: default-template-dominant median +41%, custom-template-dominant +64%, mixed +290%. Cohorts are tiny (54/135/17); CIs are wide. Suggestive only — does not survive significance test.

Reading this carefully

Three things the data does say:

1. Two coincident audience shifts in the drop window (PP rise + MM spend cliff Oct 27).

2. Both cohorts saw Q4 ROAS lift; adopters' median is lower in absolute terms.

3. AOV is flat in both cohorts (-1% / 0% medians), so any ROAS gap lives in conversion rate.

Three things the data does not say:

1. That PP caused the relative ROAS underperformance — cohorts self-selected and CIs overlap.

2. That MM cliff and PP rise are causally linked — they're time-correlated only.

3. Anything about the Max-Reach-off (Nov 19) impact — that's delivery-side, Ray's lane.
