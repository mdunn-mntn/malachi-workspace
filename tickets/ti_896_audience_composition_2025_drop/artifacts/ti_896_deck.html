TI-896 — Audience composition shift analysis

War-room investigation into the late-2025 conversion / ROAS drop — audience-side lane
Malachi Dunn · 2026-04-22

Question

Did the mix of audience types used by 2025-active advertisers shift materially during the late-2025 conversion drop, and if so — in which direction, when, and which cohorts?

Audience-side only. Conversion-rate / pixel / attribution work owned by Ray's team; customer-mix work by Will Cavey.

Product context — MNTN Match 2.0 audience tiers

Mountain Matched (MM 2.0) is the umbrella audience system. Within it, an impression's "TI Name" is determined by which scoring criteria the user satisfies. Peak Performance is one tier in this system, not a separate audience product.

Detector caveat: the "PP-enabled" detector requires both DS13 and DS19. Per the state table, true PP-tier impressions (state 5) require DS13 AND vertical match but NOT keywords. So this detector catches audience expressions that enable Mid Intent + High Intent (states 4 + 6) and by inclusion also PP. It does NOT catch a hypothetical "PP-only" audience (state 5 only). Treat the 12% number as "advertisers with audiences capable of delivering across the High Intent + PP + Mid Intent tiers" rather than "advertisers explicitly choosing PP".

Bucket overlap caveat: of 100 randomly sampled PP-detector segment expressions, 24 also contain DS2 (the MM detector flag). PP-enabled and MM detectors are partially overlapping — counting them as fully independent overstates the underlying composition shift.

Detector verification against prior conventions and the dim

  • Detector pattern matches old tickets. TI-221 and TI-270 use expression LIKE '%"data_source_id":19,%' AND expression_type_id = 2 for the same Keywords / MNTN Matched detection. Same regex convention as this analysis.
  • expression_type_id=2 filter is correct. Type 1 is legacy text format, not used by the system (Zach 2026-03-13). Cohort sample: 18,589 type-1 rows since Aug 2025, all with is_targeted=false — not used for targeting.
  • Canonical data_sources naming differs from Bryce's product labels.
    • DS2 canonical name = "MNTN First Party"; Bryce called it "Mountain Matched (MM)"
    • DS19 canonical name = "MNTN Matched"; Bryce called it "Keywords"
    Bryce's product labels used throughout this deck. Canonical names cited for cross-reference with the data dim.
  • Per-advertiser data source IDs do not appear in segment expressions. The 12,158 per-advertiser sources named {AID} - First Party Audience etc. are zero-prevalence in cohort segment-archive expressions (Apr 2026 sample). The MM detector's name LIKE '% - First Party Audience' fallback clause never fires; effective MM detection is just DS2.
  • 3P bucket is narrowly scoped per direction (DS35 = LiveRamp IP only). Broader 3P ecosystem (DS3, DS11, DS17, DS18, DS20, DS22, DS29, DS33, DS36, DS39) is excluded by this scope but is present in many PP expressions; would shift 3P numbers if included.

Cohort and method

Cohort 4,109 advertisers with ≥1 impression on any day in 2025 (~4,000 currently active)

Lookback Nov 2024 → Apr 2026, weekly grain

Targeting source archives_audience_segment_archives + archives_audiences_archives

Delivery source summarydata.sum_by_campaign_by_day

Effective windows per-campaign LEAD(update_time) capped at min(LEAD, last_active_day + 1)

The LEAD cap prevents campaigns that have stopped delivering from being credited as still running their last-attached audience indefinitely. Without it the headline "PP-enabled" advertiser number reads 21% instead of 12% (8.5pp inflation by paused campaigns).

MM cliff vs PP rise — advertiser-level overlap

Per-advertiser comparison of the week of Oct 13 2025 (pre-cliff) vs Nov 3 2025 (post-cliff). Of 1,773 advertisers active in both periods:

  • Of 135 new PP-detector advertisers in post: 78 (58%) also retained MM (DS2) presence — they added PP-enabled audiences without dropping MM.
  • Of 341 advertisers who lost MM (DS2) presence in post: only 52 (15%) gained the PP detector flag — the MM cliff is mostly NOT explained by detectable migration to PP-enabled.
  • 526 advertisers had MM (DS2) in both periods, no PP detector — stable MM-only.
  • 248 advertisers lost MM (DS2) without gaining PP detector — meaningful population dropping MM-style targeting without obvious replacement.

Most likely explanations for the unexplained MM loss: (1) advertisers migrated to MM 2.0 syntax that uses neither DS2 nor matches the PP detector, (2) genuine reductions in MM-style spend, (3) campaign restructuring under the new tier system. Audience-side data does not isolate which.

Per-bucket presence-based share over 18 months. MM stays near-universal; Keywords, 3P, CRM all decline modestly during the drop window; PP-enabled rises.

Default vs custom Peak Performance — template structure

Within PP-enabled audiences:

  • Default: audience template carries only DS13 + DS19 (minimal expression).
  • Custom: template carries DS13+DS19 plus additional DS clauses (exclusions, CRM overlays, geo, extra keyword groups).

Random sample of 1,000 PP audience templates (Oct 2025+): 39% pure DS13+DS19, 48% layered (3-4 DS ids), 14% heavily layered (≥5 DS ids).

Classifier limit: template-level structural test does not capture campaign-level layering. Formal product definition of "default" is open with the audience-tools team.

Of weekly PP-enabled adopters: ~32% run default-only templates, ~61% run custom-only, ~3% run both, ~5% unclassified (template not yet replicated to archives — CDC lag). Mix has been stable across the entire ramp.

Caveats

  • Detector specificity. The "PP-enabled" detector (DS13+DS19+rtc) catches audiences enabling Mid Intent + High Intent + PP tiers, not pure-PP-tier audiences. The 12% number is "advertisers with PP-tier-capable audiences" not "advertisers explicitly choosing PP."
  • MM/PP detector overlap. 24% of sampled PP-detector expressions also contain DS2. The two buckets are partially overlapping, not independent.
  • Cohort assignment in Track C self-selects. New PP-enabled cohort had ~1.5x higher baseline spend-weighted ROAS than non-adopters. Lift comparison does not isolate PP's effect on its own.
  • ~50% of Track C cohort has $0 order value. Medians on residual; AOV not informative for those advertisers.
  • Default-vs-custom classifier is template-level structural. Doesn't capture campaign-level layering.
  • objective_id is unreliable post-2025 TV migration; retargeting share chart uses both objective_id and funnel_level.
  • Intent score history has 35-day TTL in BQ; cannot retroactively inspect Nov 2025 PP scoring.
  • Continuing user knowledge gap: 248 advertisers lost MM (DS2) presence without gaining PP detector. Audience-side data does not explain the disposition (new MM 2.0 syntax that triggers neither detector? genuine spend reduction? campaign restructuring?).

Open questions

  • What does the new MM 2.0 audience expression look like at the database level? Does it use a different syntax that's not caught by the DS2 or DS13+DS19+rtc detectors? If so, the bucket counts here understate MM 2.0 adoption and overstate the MM cliff.
  • What drove the Oct 27 MM (DS2) spend drop? 248 of 341 affected advertisers don't show up in the PP detector. Audience-side data shows the timing but not the cause. Likely needs product-side review of what shipped that week.
  • What's the formal product definition of "default Peak Performance"? Audience-tools team (Ryan / Jordan).
  • Default vs custom PP performance with longer post window. Current 4-week Dec window is underpowered.
  • DS16 jump (1% → 35%). Real in volume but partially polluted by NOT-clause exclusions (12.5%); needs include-vs-exclude JSON parsing before publishable.
