Peak Performance launched week of Oct 6 2025. Pre-launch baseline ~1% (early-access and legacy RTC+DS13+DS19 configurations).

Near-zero through May 2025, ~1% baseline June–Sep (early-access / legacy RTC configs), sharp inflection at the Oct 6 launch. Max Reach off (Nov 19) — trajectory continued unchanged.

MM stays near-universal. Keywords/3P/CRM all flat in the drop window. Peak Performance is the only line above noise.

Long-term retargeting decline (42% → 25%) but stable in the Sep–Dec 2025 drop window.

Sep 29 → Dec 29 2025 cohort-share deltas. Peak Performance +12pp. Every other bucket within ±1pp.

Three takeaways

1. Peak Performance adoption reached 21% of 2025-active advertisers. Near-zero pre-launch, inflection at Oct 6.

2. No other audience-type shift above ~1pp noise. MM near-universal, Keywords/3P/CRM flat, retargeting share stable Sep–Dec 2025.

3. Max Reach off (Nov 19) didn’t bend any cohort-level composition curve. Peak Performance ramp continued through it uninterrupted.

Cross-reference

• Max Reach scoring off on 11/19/2025 is the closest in-time product event to the Nov onset. Conversion-angle analysis is open (Ray).

• Peak Performance scoring bug (Oct 2025) was fixed end of Oct — doesn’t fit the Nov-onward trend. Adoption ramp continued through and beyond the fix, so this isn’t a scoring-bug artefact.

• Rev/AID collapse pre-dates Peak Performance launch by a full year. Correlation only; causation requires joining with Ray’s conversion data at advertiser grain.

What the data does not say

• We count presence of each DS type in active campaign expressions. Not spend-weighted yet — a Peak Performance clause attached with zero delivery still counts.

• We don’t yet distinguish default Peak Performance recommendations from custom advertiser layering on top.

• Per-advertiser conversion delta correlated with Peak Performance adoption is the obvious next test.

Methodology

Cohort: every advertiser with ≥ 1 impression on any day in 2025 — summarydata.sum_by_campaign_by_day.

Source of targeting: dw-main-bronze.integrationprod.archives_audience_segment_archives, expression_type_id = 2, is_targeted = TRUE. 77 weekly observations, 4,111 advertisers, 93K active campaigns (as of 2026-04-22).

Classifier: regex-extract data_source_id values from expression JSON → join canonical data_sources dim → bucket to the 5 war-room categories.
