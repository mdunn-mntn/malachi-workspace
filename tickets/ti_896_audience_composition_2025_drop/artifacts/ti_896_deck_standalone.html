Audience-side lane of the late-2025 conversion / ROAS war room

Scope

Audience-side investigation: did the mix of audience types used by 2025-active advertisers shift during the late-2025 conversion drop? Cohort: 4,109 advertisers active in 2025 (~4,000 currently active). Lookback: Nov 2024 - Apr 2026, weekly. Source: archives_audience_segment_archives + sum_by_campaign_by_day.

Definitions caveat: "MM", "PP", "HI", "MI", "Max Reach" do not currently have authoritative org-wide DB-level detector definitions. Multiple sources gave conflicting interpretations (Bryce, Michelle, Nick, Zach, Alyson, Ryan). This deck uses a single concrete detector — DS13+DS19+rtc presence in segment expression — for the trajectory-rise finding, which catches all HI campaigns and some PP campaigns but does not separate them. Definition reconciliation tracked in TI-897.

"DS13+DS19+rtc presence" is the most concrete definition we have; under TI-897 we'll confirm whether this maps to PP-toggle, HI, both, or some hybrid.

DS13+DS19+rtc expressions: % of currently-active 2025-cohort advertisers with at least one such audience. Pre-launch baseline ~1%; sharp inflection at the Oct 6 2025 PP launch; reaches ~12% by April 2026. Trajectory continued through the Nov 19 Max-Reach-off event without a bend.

Of weekly DS13+DS19+rtc adopters: ~32% use pure DS13+DS19 templates (default), ~61% layer additional DS clauses (custom), ~3% run both, ~5% unclassified. Stable across the entire ramp. Whether "default" maps to MM-product-default or PP-product-default is a TI-897 question.
