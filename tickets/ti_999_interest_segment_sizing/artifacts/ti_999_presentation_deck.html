Why Buyers Pick Interest Segments Blindly
…and what TI-956 does about it

Buyers add LiveRamp interest segments without any quality signal — and ~$43M/yr of MNTN spend is allocated to the worst-performing segments at the same rate as the best.

Malachi Dunn · Targeting Infrastructure · 2026-05-28 · TI-999 Finding 15 · 30d window · 15,529 active campaigns · $40.42M

The case in one frame

1. The MNTN bidder is scored-first within pacing — falls through to unscored eligible IPs only when MM ceiling exhausts.

2. Buyers expand reach beyond MM by adding 3P-inclusion clauses — OR-additive, not narrowing.

3. Of 609 MM+3P campaigns, only 17.7% actually overflow into 3P. The other 76% pay for 3P clauses the bidder never reaches.

4. When 3P does deliver, the per-segment CVR spread is 350x top vs bottom — and buyers spend equally on each tier.

5. Conservative substitution (bottom 2 quintiles → top): ~1.6M incremental conversions/year.

§2 — The structural distinction that matters: OR vs AND

Buyers can compose positive clauses two ways. The bidder treats them very differently:

What buyer writesSemanticEligible IPsEffect on scoring
OR(MM, 3P)Union / expand(in MM) ∪ (in 3P)Brings in unscored 3P-added IPs
AND(MM, 3P)Intersect / narrow(in MM) ∩ (in 3P)Narrows MM to 3P subset; stays scored
AND(MM, NOT 3P)Exclude(in MM) ∖ (in 3P)Removes 3P-matched from MM; stays scored
Prior verbal model said "MM combines clauses with AND-intersection." The data shows that's wrong for inclusion clauses — buyers overwhelmingly use OR (union/expand), not AND (intersect/narrow).

§3 — Each pattern has a structurally distinct delivery shape

% impressions on IPs with household_score = -1 (unscored). 5/26 delivery, ~61M imps. OR expands and brings unscored; AND narrows and stays scored; AND-NOT cleans the narrowest. The user's mental model is empirically validated.

§3 — FICO case study: same MM ceiling, different overflow

FICO ran TWO campaigns simultaneously: MM_only at $41.7K → 71.5K scored imps/day. MM+3P_OR at $168.5K (4x spend) → essentially the same 60.1K scored + 236K unscored 3P-added. MM ceiling is real.

§5 — 3P-reliant prospecting is the worst cost-per-conversion

$9.77M / 30d (~$117M/yr) sits in 3P_only + 1P+3P cohorts — the two worst-performing prospecting buckets. Per-segment quality is the lever for this entire zone.

§6 — LiveRamp segments have 350x CVR spread

1,005 LiveRamp dscids with ≥100K weighted impressions support. Top quintile: 0.140% CVR, $53/conv. Bottom: 0.0004% CVR, $14,525/conv. Buyers spend equally on each tier — they have no quality signal.

§6 — Counterfactual: ~1.6M incremental conversions / year (50% sub)

Bottom-2 quintile spend ($3.65M / 30d, ~$43.8M/yr) currently delivers 2.7K conversions. At top-quintile CVR (0.140%), would deliver 274K. Conservative 50% substitution → ~1.6M incremental conv/year.

§7 — Recommendations

Phase 1: Ship TI-956 LiveRamp scoring on a schedule (Alex Knorr framework → GCS, Macie wires admin UI). Top-N segment picker by composite quality.

Phase 2: Surface MM ceiling in campaign-setup UI. "Your budget exceeds MM segment X's ceiling — overflow $(Y-Z) will route to 3P, pick high-quality segments here →"

Phase 3: Diagnostic for the 76% below-ceiling cohort. "Your 3P clause isn't being reached. Options: remove / scale spend / replace with broader MM."

Phase 4: Address the 1P+3P anti-pattern. 252 campaigns / $4.53M at $192/conv (3.8x worse than MM_only). Education + product.

Appendix — methodology & sources

Window: 30d ending 2026-05-28. Active campaign = ≥1 impression in window. All numbers reconcile to TI-999 Finding 15 in summary.md.

AST parse: JS UDF in queries/ti_999_clause_polarity_ast.sql + ti_999_finding15_pass12_or_vs_and_inclusion.sql. Tracks per-leaf OR-group-id for OR-vs-AND disambiguation.

Score distributions: from cost_impression_log.model_params on 2026-05-26 (~61M impressions). household_score, advertiser_household_score, realtime_conquest_score per impression.

Per-segment CVR: equal-attribution proxy (campaign metrics ÷ N dscids). Matches TI-956 default target_weight='equal'. True causal isolation requires Alex's Performance axis.

Caveats: descriptive not causal; single-day score-distribution snapshot; equal-attribution can over/underweight specific dscids; quintile rank stability over time not verified.
