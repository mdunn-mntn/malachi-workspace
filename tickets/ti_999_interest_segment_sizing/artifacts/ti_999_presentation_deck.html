Four questions, four answers

Q1. What counts as an interest segment? Three active third-party providers: LiveRamp, ShareThis, Dstillery.
Q2. How many? 218,782 active dscids — LiveRamp = 97.6%.
Q3. How stale? LiveRamp 99.6% fresh. ShareThis & Dstillery 100% >2yr.
Q4. How do they perform? No-3P wins. Fresh beats stale. Mixing 3P signals hurts.

Plus: where is the spend concentrated, and how much of 3P's reach is already covered by CRM?

Sizing — 34.6% of prospecting touches 3P

$8.59M / 30d through 3P → ~$103M/yr. Fresh LiveRamp = $4.03M; stale-3P touching = $4.56M (~$55M/yr).

Stale-3P exposure is concentrated

Top 15 = 56% of stale-3P prospecting spend. Top 5 = ~40%. ElevenLabs $0.72M/mo leads. WGU absent — their stale-3P was retargeting.

3P usage is flat across tiers (~40-56%)

Every customer segment uses 3P in prospecting. Enterprise = 34.3% of prospecting spend (vs 50.6% of total spend — retargeting concentrates more in enterprise).

3P providers overlap heavily with each other

64% of Dstillery IPs are already in LiveRamp; 47% of ShareThis IPs are in LiveRamp. Layering multiple 3P providers brings less incremental reach than buying them suggests.

Where do advertisers pick on the activity scale?

Median LiveRamp choice at 77th activity percentile; 100% in top quartile. ShareThis 65th; Dstillery 59th. Buyers do self-select toward active segments — but activity is a proxy. Real "switch to top-N lift" awaits TI-956 composite scores.

What this means for TI-956 scoring

1. The bigger question may be "should they use 3P at all?"

No-3P prospecting converts 2.1x better. Scoring framework's strongest application could be flagging campaigns to drop 3P entirely, not just ranking alternatives.

2. Phase 1 (LiveRamp-only) is the right starting point.

LiveRamp is 97.6% of the catalog and fresh — the framework's other 8 axes (uniqueness, specificity, activity, performance) are where the value lies.

3. Phase 2 = ShareThis + Dstillery. Then mix-bucket investigation.

~$55M/yr touches stale-3P; fresh LiveRamp converts 44% better. Mix bucket is worst — likely the bidder's intersection logic biasing toward heavily-tagged IPs.

4. Concentration enables an incremental rollout.

Top 5 prospecting advertisers (ElevenLabs, Gainbridge, Northern Tool, Taskrabbit, Windstream) cover ~40% of stale-3P exposure. Pilot there.

Methodology caveats

ItemCaveat
DS reference detectionRegex captures every DS in the expression incl. exclusion clauses ("op":"not"). "Uses X" means "expression references X."
KPI comparisonsDescriptive, not causal. Selection effects (vertical, funnel position, advertiser sophistication) confound.
updated_dateMeasures taxonomy freshness, not data-feed freshness. No positive freshness signal for ShareThis/Dstillery.
"Only stale 3P" small n52 campaigns; precise % is noisy. Direction (stale < fresh) is robust.
IP overlapSingle-day, universe-level. Per-campaign overlap differs.
Lift estimatesOrder-of-magnitude. Real measurement needs a controlled comparison post-TI-956.
