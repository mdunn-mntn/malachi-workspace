Valuation model

A vendor is worth the margin its data lets us keep, minus what the data costs us. A vendor makes us money one way: its data lets us serve impressions we bill to clients. Only incremental impressions count — if we would have served the impression anyway (own free sources, or another vendor already on the roster), the vendor gets no credit. Counting everything a vendor "touches" lets every vendor claim the same impression; measured, the 8 paid vendors' touched claims sum to 6.39× the impressions actually served.

Net Kept Marginᵥ = (Iᵥ ÷ 1,000) × CPM × M − Dᵥ

Iᵥ   — unique (IP × Domain × Date) impressions served only because of vendor v
        (net of free sources; leave-one-out against the rest of the paid roster)
CPM — client-facing price per 1,000 served impressions  ·  M — gross margin, % of revenue
Dᵥ   — data cost paid to vendor v, same window  ·  positive → pays for itself

Definitions

  • sources — paid metered: 24 Justuno, 28 33Across, 33 Sovrn, 36 Cybba, 40 33A API ($0.50 CPM). Flat: 25 5x5, 26 Predactiv, 39 Klickly. Free internal: 23 guid_log, 30 augmentor.
  • triple — distinct (IP, REG_DOMAIN(url), date); usable = domain consumable by DS13 or DS19. IPv4 only.
  • touched — won imps (CIL, val wk) on IPs the source delivered in 37d. Cohorts overlap: the same imp counts under every source that delivered its IP (avg served IP held by ~6.7 of 10 sources), so the column cannot be summed — Σ across the 8 paid = 6.39× actual imps.
  • unique / Iᵥ — won imps on IPs neither free log delivered (q8b solo cohort = vendor as only paid source).
  • tier — per-IP MAX(household_score), val wk: HI=10000, PP=8000, high-grad 6666–9999 (Fangorn DS46 graduated bands; DS13 v1 pins the constants), mid, max-reach, unscored ≤0.
