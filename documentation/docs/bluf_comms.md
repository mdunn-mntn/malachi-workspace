# BLUF — Bottom Line Up Front

Lead with the bottom line. Conclusion, recommendation, or ask in the first sentence. Support after, and only what changes what the reader decides or does.

Same principle you already use under other names: the deck's **Power Line**, the Jira **Answer line**, the Terse Comms "one rule." BLUF is that rule applied to live, human-facing comms — Slack, email, standup, meetings, anything you relay.

## The pattern

```
[Bottom line: the answer / recommendation / ask]
[1-3 supporting facts, only if they change the decision]
[The ask or next step, if there is one]
```

## The 5-second check

If the reader stopped after your first sentence, would they still have the decision-relevant point? If no, rewrite the opener.

## Before / after

**Standup**
- ✗ "I spent yesterday digging into the MM classifier backfill, ran into a merge edge case, looked at a few options, and I think we're mostly there."
- ✓ "MM classifier is live in prod as of Friday. One follow-up: full-model backfills land at merge, not next cron. No blockers."

**Slack to a director**
- ✗ "Wanted to share some analysis on the vendor eval. There's a lot here but the summary is that across the lenses we looked at, none of them really break even..."
- ✓ "None of the 5 vendors break even at $0.50/unit — the result holds across every valuation lens. Detail + workbook below."

**Exec email**
- ✗ "Following up on the goal-attainment question from Tuesday. We pulled the numbers and did some segmentation..."
- ✓ "63% of customers hit their goal (UMM 71 / MM 66 / SMB 58). Method and full cut attached."

## When NOT to bury the lead

- Bad news: state it first, then context. Don't warm up to it.
- A recommendation: give it before the reasoning, then defend it.
- A question you need answered: ask it first, then supply the background.

## The failure mode to avoid

Chronological order. You did the work in discovery order; don't present it that way. The reader wants the destination first, then the map — not to ride along through every turn you took.
