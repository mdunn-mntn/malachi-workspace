Self-review tone:
- **Openings**: 2 sentences, directly map to rubric criteria using natural language. Confident and specific. NOT philosophical, NOT navel-gazing, NOT "a big part of my value is..." style.
- **Task descriptions**: Specific, quantified, end with business impact. Don't over-explain or pad. State what was done and why it matters.
- **Improvement areas**: Aspirational, not deficit-framing. "I want to present more" not "I don't present enough."
- **Overall**: Should sound like a competent person wrote it, not like an LLM generated it. When in doubt, keep it shorter and more direct.
- The submitted review #1 is the gold standard for tone — compare against it when writing review #2.
