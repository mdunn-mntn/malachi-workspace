**Verified empirically 2026-08-28 (AUDI-1241, feeds `airflow_optimizer/bq_profile.py`):**

- **`BigQueryInsertJobOperator` stamps labels `airflow-dag` / `airflow-task` on every job it
  submits**; direct inserts also carry a `job_id` of the form `airflow_<dag>_<task>_<ts>`, so the
  dag/task is recoverable from either field.
- **Jobs run by a python client INSIDE a task carry NO labels** — label-based attribution misses
  them entirely. *(2026-09-03, AUDI-1278: unless the caller passes them; airflow-ti [#1278](https://github.com/SteelHouse/airflow-ti/pull/1278) adds `airflow_job_labels()` at the 8 python-client call sites, and Spark-BigQuery-connector jobs are the same class, see below.)*
- **airflow-ti / camperbid jobs bill in `dw-main-bronze`** (that is the job project to query).
- **CORRECTED 2026-08-28 (first prod run): `JOBS_BY_USER` does NOT give the sweep the fleet's
  history.** The earlier claim ("the fleet SA can profile its own history with zero IAM change")
  was wrong: the optimizer DAG impersonates `spark-optimizer@mntn-prj-prod-00`
  (`dags/spark_optimizer_daily.py` `SERVICE_ACCOUNT`), which submits NONE of the fleet's jobs, so
  `JOBS_BY_USER` returned an empty day. Correct approach (now in code, airflow-ti#1247):
  `INFORMATION_SCHEMA.JOBS_BY_PROJECT` filtered to `user_email IN (OPTIMIZER_BQ_SAS)` (default
  `airflow-ti-prod` + `airflow-camperbid-prod`), which needs `bigquery.jobUser` +
  `resourceViewer` on `dw-main-bronze` (mntn-devops#5160, open).
- **Access denied to `malachi@mountain.com`:** `JOBS_BY_ORGANIZATION` and
  `mntn-prj-prod-00:INFORMATION_SCHEMA.JOBS_BY_PROJECT`.
- **THE OPTIMIZER'S BQ SURFACE IS SCOPED BY SERVICE ACCOUNT, NOT BY TEAM — by design, not a leak
  (settled 2026-09-03, AUDI-1278/AUDI-1290).** `include/spark_optimizer/bq_profile.py` `SAS` defaults
  to `airflow-ti-prod@mntn-prj-prod-00.iam.gserviceaccount.com` **and**
  `airflow-camperbid-prod@mntn-prj-prod-00.iam.gserviceaccount.com` (override: env `OPTIMIZER_BQ_SAS`).
  **The SPARK surface excludes other teams by team label (`phs.TEAM`); the BQ surface never did.** That
  asymmetry is the whole answer to "why is the sweep looking at another team's jobs" — it is why the
  sweep flagged the camperbid `bos__spend` / `flight_metrics_per2388` work and why that produced
  **airflow-camperbid PR #580**. Malachi had raised this with two different teams; this closes it. If
  you ever want the BQ surface team-scoped, the lever is `OPTIMIZER_BQ_SAS`, not a code change.

**Verified 2026-09-02/03 (AUDI-1278, hackathon epic AUDI-1290; record in `tickets/audi_1290_pipeline_optimization_hackathon/audi_1278_bq_job_labels/summary.md`):**

- **What the "unattributed" bucket IS.** `bq_profile.py` (`PROFILE_SQL`) reads `dw-main-bronze.region-us-central1.INFORMATION_SCHEMA.JOBS_BY_PROJECT` filtered to `user_email IN (airflow-ti-prod@, airflow-camperbid-prod@)` and buckets `airflow-dag=''` as `unattributed`, so every job in it was submitted by one of our two Airflow deployments (the 2026-09-02 "jobs outside the airflow-launched set" hypothesis is refuted). 7 days 2026-08-26..09-01: **612 jobs/day, 1,109.9 slot-h/day**; 96.9% of the slot-hours are the four `bos__spend` Spark scripts in `SteelHouse/airflow-camperbid` (`spark_scripts/bos/campaign_flight_end_cost.py`, `campaign_group_flight_end_cost.py`, `sum_by_private_marketplace_by_hour.py`, `campaign_utc_yesterday_costs_impressions_by_hour.py`) reading through the Spark-BigQuery connector on Dataproc Serverless batches; `win_rate_hourly` 29 slot-h/day; airflow-ti 147 jobs/day, 3.9 slot-h/day (all of it the `dlv_pattern_identification` CTAS). The ledger never records the bucket by design (`bq_profile.reports()` attaches no finding to it, `ledger.record()` skips finding-less reports, pinned by `test_heavy_task_is_a_finding_and_unattributed_is_not`); it lives only in the daily GCS report `gs://mntn-data-archive-prod/optimizer/optimizer_bq_<date>.md`, which exists from 2026-08-28 and reconciles to `JOBS_BY_PROJECT` within rounding on every overlapping day (08-28..09-01 mean 606 jobs/day, 1,104.7 slot-h/day). That row is the before/after surface (decision 0007).
- **Spark-BigQuery connector jobs (`viewsEnabled` + `query` reads, and load jobs) carry no labels and show as UUID-id SELECT jobs with an anonymous-dataset destination.** The connector submits the job itself, so `BigQueryInsertJobOperator` never sees it. Fix = Spark conf `spark.datasource.bigquery.bigQueryJobLabel.<key>=<value>`: `SparkBigQueryConfig.parseBigQueryLabels` (master `a2cf326`, present on the 0.42 line Dataproc Serverless 2.3 bundles) strips the `bigQueryJobLabel.` prefix over the global `spark.datasource.bigquery.*` options, per-read options override, and the result labels every connector-initiated query and load job whichever helper builds the reader. For camperbid that is two Jinja-rendered keys in `dag_utils/google.py` (`run_dataproc_serverless` `runtime_config.properties` and `DataprocConfig.asJson` `pyspark_job.properties`; `DataprocCreateBatchOperator.template_fields` includes `batch`, `DataprocInstantiateInlineWorkflowTemplateOperator`'s includes `template`, nested dict strings render recursively): `...airflow-dag = {{ dag.dag_id | lower }}` and `...airflow-task = {{ task.task_id | lower | replace('.', '-') }}`. Hand-off (diff + Slack send-draft + dev check query) drafted for `@SteelHouse/pacing` + `@SteelHouse/performance-ml` (CODEOWNERS `*`), user sends; not merged as of 2026-09-03.
- **Python-client labels, both forms verified from source:** `client.query(sql, job_config=bigquery.QueryJobConfig(labels={...}))`; and `BigQueryHook.get_df(sql, configuration={"labels": {...}})` (the hook forwards kwargs to `pandas_gbq.read_gbq` untouched, `_transform_read_gbq_configuration` only rewrites `query.timeoutMs`; pandas_gbq 0.34.1 builds the job with `QueryJobConfig.from_api_repr`, top-level `labels` honoured). The airflow-ti helper `include/util/bq_job_labels.py::airflow_job_labels(context=None)` mirrors `BigQueryInsertJobOperator._add_job_labels` (`dag_id.lower()`, `task_id.lower().replace('.', '-')`, both must match `^[\w-]{0,63}$`, else `{}`; `{}` outside a task because `airflow.sdk.get_current_context()` raises `RuntimeError` there), so client-submitted and operator-submitted jobs group under one `airflow-dag` key in `bq_profile.py`. Shipped at 8 call sites in airflow-ti #1278 (open 2026-09-03; dev-deployment run not done, verification = the first post-merge daily report).
- **Not every airflow-ti BQ job bills in dw-main-bronze.** The three `get_df` DAGs (`set_gaclid_enabled_flag`, `blocked_ip_addresses_export`, `blocked_guids_export`) use `gcp_conn_id="google_cloud_default"` and produced zero jobs under `airflow-ti-prod@` in dw-main-bronze over the 7 days; they bill to that connection's project, so the optimizer never sees them, labels or not (which project: open, ticket §8.4).
- **Residual camperbid python-client jobs stay unattributed after the Spark-property fix** (~24/day, <1 slot-h/day): the `autotof_morning_pass` kedro job (SQL in olympus `autotof-v0.17.0`, run on an ephemeral Dataproc cluster), `spark_scripts/initial_bvp_V7/bvp_data_refresh_v7.py::load_bq`, the `external.camperbid_prod__hhst_v#__campaign_bucket` MERGE (source file not found in airflow-camperbid or olympus) and 7 LOAD jobs/day (source not found; `bigquery_save_table` uses `writeMethod=direct`, so it is not the LOAD source). Listed in the hand-off for the owners.
- **Mode never shows the bucket:** report `e81786de8403` "BigQuery cost by task" reads `optimizer.optimization_ledger` `surface='bq'` only. A `JOBS_BY_PROJECT` query in Mode would need `bigquery.jobs.listAll` (`roles/bigquery.resourceViewer`) for `mode-analytics@dw-main-bronze` via mntn-devops (`iam_bronze_extras.tf` line 158 grants only the layer reader role); recorded as an option for the user, not requested. **(Superseded 2026-09-03, AUDI-1316: the access is ALREADY held.)**

**CORRECTION 2026-09-03 (AUDI-1316, verified empirically + adversarially):**

The prior claim inferred "layer reader only" from the terraform without reading the role's permission list; the new evidence reads the permission list plus the live project policy, which is a better evidence class. `mode-analytics@dw-main-bronze` holds exactly one role on dw-main-bronze, `organizations/104640274931/roles/medallion_bronze_reader`, defined by `google_organization_iam_custom_role.layer_reader` in `terragrunt/gcp/resources/mntn/dplat/modules/dw-medallion-layer/iam.tf:19-43`. That role includes **`bigquery.jobs.listAll`, `bigquery.jobs.create`, `bigquery.jobs.get`, `bigquery.jobs.list`** (lines 29, 26-28), plus `bigquery.tables.getData`, `bigquery.reservations.use`, and storage/cloudsql reads. It is a broad read role, not a table-read role. The spark-optimizer precedent (AUDI-1241) needed a grant because `spark-optimizer@mntn-prj-prod-00` held NO role on dw-main-bronze at all before the grant; the two cases are not comparable. Residual risk: `gcloud iam roles describe medallion_bronze_reader --organization=104640274931` is PERMISSION_DENIED for `malachi@mountain.com`, so terraform-versus-live drift would surface as a 403 on the first Mode run; the standby grant `artifacts/audi_1316_standby_grant.yaml` exists for exactly that case.
