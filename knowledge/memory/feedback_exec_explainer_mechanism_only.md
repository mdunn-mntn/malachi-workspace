**When building an exec-facing methodology explainer (one-sheeter, single slide, primer for leadership), explain HOW the method works and stop — do not put the results on the page.** (Malachi, 2026-08-07, AUDI-1172 ghost-bid one-sheeter for the Mark conversation: "the biggest thing is not to explain what they saw, but just to explain how it works. High-level, not too in the weeds, info graphs even better.")

**Why:** the explainer's job is to make the audience trust the method before a results conversation; baking a results snapshot in dates the artifact (live rollups drift), invites debate about the numbers instead of the method, and couples a reusable teaching asset to one analysis.

**How to apply:**
- Structure: the measurement problem → mechanism diagram → why to trust it → how to read the output (use ILLUSTRATIVE numbers, clearly labeled) → footer pointer to where the real results live.
- Info graphics over prose; KPI tiles carry methodology stats (e.g. 10% holdout, advertiser coverage, $0 extra media), not findings.
- Phrasing accuracy for ghost-bid materials: ghost = the bid it would have PLACED (wins are never known); the compared arm is the ad-eligible group vs holdout (ITT), never "served vs ghost"; name the 7-day visit window as the conservatism.
- Reusable instance: [[reference_select_vs_nonselect_incrementality]] artifacts.
