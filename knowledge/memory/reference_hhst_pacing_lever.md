The Household Score Threshold (HHST) — the min household_score the bidder will serve — is **not static**. For actively-managed advertisers it is changed constantly by pacing/optimization logic (HexClad prospecting: 51 changes Jan–May 2026, range −1 to 10000). **Each gate change inverts delivery the NEXT DAY:** gate→10000 = ~100% High-Intent overnight; gate→0/−1 = ~12% HI + rest unscored, and daily impressions jump. This is the mechanism behind STEEP/RAPID MoM/daily performance swings — config flips, NOT gradual audience decline or model degradation.

- **threshold=0 or negative (−1, −100) = NO gate (serve anyone)**, not "gate at score 0."
- **A common cause of "gate at 0": short-flight HHST=0 — but it's a MANUAL practice, not automated (Tofer, AUDI-1070 2026-07-01).** **Tofer manually sets HHST=0 for every campaign/flight that launches at ≤72h** (deliverability/pacing); official since early 2025, unofficial for years before. It is NOT an automated system trigger, and it's INCONSISTENT — "it is only me doing it… there are flights/campaigns I have missed." So a 0-gate on a short flight is likely-but-not-guaranteed. This explains why some short-flight advertisers get zeroed (HexClad → gate removed, delivery left HI) and others don't (Caraway ran short flights but stayed 85–99% HI — Tofer missed them). Check flight START/END from **`silver.core.flights`** (flight_id, campaign_group_id, start_time, end_time, budget, status_id; status 3=active/completed, 8=superseded) — a "flight" is per campaign_GROUP and each budget/schedule edit spawns a new flight row, so a short flight can be a mid-schedule tweak, not a fresh launch. Do NOT infer flight length from consecutive active-days (merges flights). **Quantified (AUDI-1070 HexClad group 93373, 73 flights): short flights (≤72h) run ungated 45% of their days vs 28% for long — a real tendency, NOT 1:1.** To SYNC gate+spend+flights on one axis: daily gate (archive, QUALIFY by day) + daily spend (`sum_by_campaign_by_day`) + flight spans (`silver.core.flights`, join campaign_group_id, `TIMESTAMP_DIFF(end,start,HOUR)`=dur, `budget`, `status_id=3`). Chart `hexclad_gate_spend_flights.png`. Biggest damage = holiday MEGA-flights ($112k/$165k/$409k/$180k budgets Nov-Jan) running ungated at ~$100k/day.
- Pacing RAMPS (gate +300/day 3333→…→6666 then reset to 0) = a controller opening the gate to find fill when spend outruns the HI pool.
- **The automated setter is `ddm.hhst_generate_recommendation` (DDM / Redshift, hourly) — objective = PURE PACING/budget-fill, NOT value:** picks the HIGHEST household_score threshold whose remaining winnable IP population still spends the remaining daily budget at recent cost-per-win; lowers only to keep pacing; cap 6666, floor 1; no visit/conversion/lift term. Archives `actual` vs `recommended` to `ddm.archive_hhst_recommendation` (= human-vs-bot divergence); scoped to `ddm.test_hhst_campaigns`. The sub-HI inventory it admits to pace is ~5x costlier per observed visit than HI (correlational) — see [[reference_hhst_efficiency_sizing]].
- Source: `silver.archives.household_score_threshold_archives` (advertiser_id, campaign_id, threshold, update_time). Collapse to change-events with `LAG(threshold) OVER (PARTITION BY campaign_id ORDER BY update_time)` keeping threshold!=prev. Live values: `silver.dso.household_score_thresholds`. Catalog entry added.
- **The gate BINDS ~perfectly** on `household_score` (NOT advertiser_household_score). Verified AUDI-1070: sustained gate=10000 → 99.99% of gated-path imps are exactly 10000 (0–1 non-HI imp/day out of ~150K; residual = ~1-day flip-day propagation lag). So "non-HI under a high gate" is never a leak — rule out these instead: (1) **RTC (Real-Time Conquest)** bypasses the gate by design (fires first, serves conquest HHs regardless of score; detect model_params LIKE "%realtime_conquest_score=10000%"; ~8% of HexClad Jan-2026 imps, 35% HI — RTC was OFF 2025, ON 2026); (2) **monthly aggregation** blending no-gate days with gated days; (3) **multiple campaigns** at different gates. advertiser_household_score diverges ~10% from household_score in both directions — use household_score for gate/HI reasoning.
- **Rule:** ANY YoY/MoM advertiser-KPI comparison must overlay the HHST gate history — a "decline" is often the time-average of a gate held loose (chasing spend) punctuated by brief HI-only windows. Canonical: AUDI-1070 HexClad. **The gate was THRASHED, not "set to 0 and never reverted"** (that phrasing was WRONG — corrected per Johnny's pushback + archive pull, 2026-07-01). Flagship 446801, 66 changes over the year: gated ~6666 Jul–Oct (93–98% HI) → **removed mid-Nov (~Nov 14)** to chase 20× holiday volume (HI → 11–15%) → **RESTORED to 10000 Jan 5** (delivery recovered to ~80% HI = **natural experiment** proving the gate is the lever) → **off again Feb 5** (31%) → oscillating Mar–Jun (Fangorn ramps interrupted by drops to 0). Delivery HI-share tracks the gate EVERY time. The damage = repeated **ungated stretches** (worst the Nov–Dec blowout). Caraway's gate mostly HELD (over-scaling story instead, not gate-removal). **Lesson: never claim a gate "never reverted" from a dashboard glance — pull the per-campaign daily archive (QUALIFY row_number by day) first; fluctuation is the norm for managed advertisers.** Chart `hexclad_gate_thrash.png`.
