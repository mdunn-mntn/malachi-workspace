Kale is sharpening TI team focus toward **incrementality** as the primary optimization objective (2026-03-31 1x1).

**Why:** Purely exploitative intent targeting makes MNTN's incrementality reports look bad — highest-intent users are also targeted by Google/Meta, so MNTN's marginal lift is hard to demonstrate. Fangorn's current approach (flat 10,000 score for all high-intent IPs) is 100% exploit, 0% explore.

**How to apply:** When working on Fangorn, BUK, feature store, or any targeting work — frame analysis and recommendations through an incrementality lens, not just IVR/ROAS. Kale will come with a concrete plan to narrow the focus. Alex Floor, Mike Dole, and Kale are covering product leadership during the GPM search (Michelle departed).
