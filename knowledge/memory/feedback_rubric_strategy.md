Self-review goal is all 4s at current level. The rubric resets after promotion (you'd score 3s at the new level), so there's a real tradeoff between promotion and staying put with consistent top scores.

**Why:** Compensation strategy — consistent top-of-level raises can compound better than early promotion depending on raise percentages. Promotion also raises expectations. The self-review is the written defense that maps work to rubric criteria.

**How to apply:** Every self-review entry must clearly and unambiguously map to a 4 on the rubric at the current level. No room for "could be a 3 or 4" — make the case obvious. The self-review is binary: hit 4s or get nothing. Frame work against Speed/Craft/Adaptability criteria explicitly.
