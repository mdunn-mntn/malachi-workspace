## How to Apply
When building presentations for this audience:
- Kale: scrutinizes methodology, wants to rip it apart before sending up
- Paulo: wants to understand "why the system behaves this way" — frame findings as system insights
- Richard: wants ship-it results, not hedged analysis. Bold statements backed by data. Past BUK experiments failed his bar.
- Keep it under 5 minutes for Loom format. High-level: what we did, results, next steps.
