**Key points:**
- Three skills: Speed (Delivery/Volume), Craft (Technical Quality), Adaptability (Responsiveness/Ownership)
- Five levels: Associate, Mid, Senior, Staff, Principal
- Promotion = 3s-4s at current level, all 3s at next level
