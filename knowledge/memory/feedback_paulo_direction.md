Paulo (VP, Kale's boss) answered "Why are you on the payroll?" on 2026-03-18:

1. **Primary role**: Be the best at explaining why things are happening. Explain system behavior
   with data and an understanding of the system dynamics. Identify incongruity between expected
   and actual system behavior. Explain complex environments clearly.

2. **How to be most useful**: Be the go-to person when anybody doesn't understand why the complex
   ecosystem is behaving the way it is. Start with mastering targeting, move outward.

3. **How to make leadership look good**: Equip Alyson with insightful, crisp summaries so that
   when Paulo asks her about something (e.g., NTB status), she has what she needs. Paulo says
   "wow, great explanation, no further questions" and Alyson says "thanks, Malachi is crushing
   this project." Then when Paulo needs the brawniest brain for an important project, Malachi
   is the first person on his mind.

4. **Longer-term growth**: Discover opportunities that are solid bets and feasible to de-risk.
   Prove that things are worth the investment. This is harder than #1 — get good at #1 first.

Implications for self-review:
- Frame work as "explaining why the system behaves this way" not just "I investigated X"
- Emphasize how deliverables equipped Alyson/Kale with crisp answers for leadership
- Targeting mastery is the current domain focus — prioritize targeting-related investigations
- The longer-term goal (opportunity discovery + de-risking) is aspirational but worth tracking
