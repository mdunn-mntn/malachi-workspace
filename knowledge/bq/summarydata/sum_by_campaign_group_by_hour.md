# summarydata.sum_by_campaign_group_by_hour

## Purpose
Pre-aggregated hourly performance rollup at the **campaign-group** level (the group of campaigns
under an advertiser, e.g. a Prospecting group). It is the **hour-grain** sibling of
`summarydata.sum_by_campaign_group_by_day`, carrying the same measure surface but bucketed to the
hour. Reach for it when you need intraday cadence (dayparting, hourly pacing, hour-of-day patterns)
at the campaign-group grain, or a campaign-group rollup that stays **fresh through the current day**
(partitions extend to 2026-07-20; unlike the historical ~17-day-lag note on the day-grain
siblings). For campaign- or advertiser-level grain, use `sum_by_campaign_by_day` /
`sum_by_advertiser_by_day` (day only). For raw hour-grain facts that never lag, the underlying
`summarydata.{impression_facts, visit_facts, conversion_facts, spend_facts}` / `all_facts` fact
tables are the freshest source.

## Grain & keys
- **Grain:** one row per **advertiser_id × campaign_group_id × hour** (`hour` = TIMESTAMP truncated
  to the top of the hour, UTC). Empirically verified unique: on 2026-04-13, rows = distinct
  (advertiser_id, campaign_group_id, hour) = 97,094, spanning 24 distinct hours × 1,763 advertisers
  × 4,660 campaign groups. No duplicate/soft-delete rows.
- **Key(s) / join columns:** `advertiser_id` → `bronze.integrationprod.advertisers.id`;
  `campaign_group_id` → `bronze.integrationprod.campaign_groups.id` (which carries `product_id`
  1=PTV / 2=Select / 3=QuickFrame). There is **no campaign_id** here — this is already collapsed
  above the campaign level, so per-campaign `objective_id`/`funnel_level` splits are not recoverable
  from this table.

## Column meanings (only the non-obvious ones)
**HLL reach sketches (BYTES — NOT integers):** `uniques`, `site_visitors`, `new_site_visitors`,
`existing_site_visitors`, `new_users_reached`, `existing_users_reached`, and the `competing_*` /
`probattr_*` `*_site_visitors` variants are HLL++ sketches, not counts. **Never SUM or CAST them.**
Extract with `HLL_COUNT.MERGE(col)` (across rows) or `HLL_COUNT.EXTRACT(col)` (single row). Verified
on 2026-04-13: `HLL_COUNT.MERGE(uniques)` = 15,342,540 reached, `HLL_COUNT.MERGE(site_visitors)` =
708,187. BQ's HLL engine lands ~1–2% under ClickHouse's `uniqArrayMerge` on the same sketch — reach
brackets are approximate, spend/impressions/attributed counts are exact.

**Spend / cost columns (NUMERIC dollars):** `media_spend`, `data_spend`, `platform_spend` are the
**revenue/billing-side** components — total advertiser spend = sum of the three (verified magnitudes
2026-04-13: platform 754K + media 566K + data 50K). `media_cost` is the separate **cost-side**
figure (what MNTN pays for media; 470K < media_spend 566K on the same slice) and is the basis for
"% to cap" calculations. Note this hour table **retains `media_cost`** even though the BQ migration
dropped the cost-side duplicates (`data_cost`, `fee_cost`, `partner_cost`, `legacy_spend`) from the
day-grain siblings (TI-832). `video_spend` (BIGNUMERIC) is the CTV/video slice of spend.

**Attribution-variant measures — SEPARATE models, NOT additive buckets; never SUM across families
(verified AUDI-1070):**
- **Default:** `views`, `clicks`, `view_conversions`, `click_conversions`, `view_order_value`,
  `click_order_value` — the last-touch headline. **INCLUDES CTV** (a CTV view-conversion is a
  `view_conversion`).
- **`competing_*`:** industry_standard **first-touch** variant. UI "Verified Visits" =
  `clicks + views + competing_views`; UI order value adds `competing_view_order_value`. These are
  the columns that close the reported-ROAS gap for `industry_standard` advertisers.
- **`competing_last_tv_touch_*`** (`competing_last_tv_touch_views`, `_view_conversions`,
  `_view_order_value`): the TV-only last-touch model under the competing/industry_standard lens —
  credits the *same* conversion to a *different* campaign group. **Not a subset, not additive** with
  the default cols (adding them double-counts dual-exposed conversions). **NOTE:** this hour table
  carries ONLY the `competing_` TV-touch columns; the plain `last_tv_touch_*` and `last_touch_*`
  families that exist on the day-grain sibling are **absent** here.
- **`probattr_*`:** probabilistic attribution mirrors (`probattr_view_conversions`,
  `probattr_view_order_value`, `probattr_last_touch_*`, `probattr_competing_*`, …) — the right
  surface for probabilistically-attributed ROAS/CPA modeling.
- **`competing_visit_assists`, `competing_conversions_assist_view*`:** multi-touch assist credit.

**Viewability / delivery (INT counts):** `view_impressions` = impressions eligible for viewability
measurement; `view_viewed` = of those, measured viewable; `view_untrackable` = could not be
measured. `display_impressions` vs `video_impressions` = display vs CTV/video impression split.
`vast_start / vast_firstquartile / vast_midpoint / vast_thirdquartile / vast_complete` = VAST video
quartile completion counts (VCR = `vast_complete / vast_start`).

## Joins & relationships
- **→ `bronze.integrationprod.campaign_groups`** on `campaign_group_id = campaign_groups.id`
  (dim, 1:1 per group; filter `deleted=FALSE AND is_test=FALSE`). Adds `product_id` (PTV/Select/QF),
  `advertiser_id`, group name. Fan-out: none (dim side unique on `id`).
- **→ `bronze.integrationprod.advertisers`** on `advertiser_id = advertisers.id` (dim, 1:1). Use
  `company_name` for a reliable advertiser label.
- **↔ `summarydata.sum_by_campaign_group_by_day`** — same entity, day-grain. Summing 24 hourly rows
  reproduces the daily row for additive measures (impressions, spend, conversion counts); HLL
  sketches must be `HLL_COUNT.MERGE`d, not summed, to match.
- **No campaign-level join** — `campaign_id` is absent (rolled up). To split by
  `objective_id`/`funnel_level` you must drop to `sum_by_campaign_by_day` (day grain) or the
  campaign-grain fact tables and join `campaign_id → campaigns.campaign_id`.

## Gotchas
- **BYTES columns are HLL sketches** — the single most common error is treating `uniques` /
  `*_site_visitors` / `*_users_reached` as integers. `HLL_COUNT.MERGE` only. (See Column meanings.)
- **Never SUM across attribution variants** (default / `competing_*` / `last_tv_touch_*` /
  `probattr_*` / `*_assist_*`) — they are separate models of the *same* conversions and double-count
  when added. Pick one family per calculation.
- **`hour` is UTC.** Bucketing is top-of-hour UTC; dayparting analyses must convert to the target
  timezone. Partitions can show a `hour` date one day ahead of "today" (max partition 2026-07-20 on
  2026-07-19) due to UTC roll-forward — expected, not stale.
- **Freshness:** this hour table currently extends through the current day; the ~17-day-lag
  staleness that historically affected the day-grain rollups (`sum_by_campaign_group_by_day`,
  `sum_by_advertiser_by_day`, verified 2026-05-01) is **not** present here — but always
  `SELECT MAX(hour)` on a bounded recent window before trusting a recency claim.
- **`require_partition_filter = true`** — any query without a `hour` predicate errors outright.
- **SQLMesh view indirection:** query the clean name `summarydata.sum_by_campaign_group_by_hour`;
  it resolves to the versioned physical `summarydata__sum_by_campaign_group_by_hour__<hash>` which
  changes on rebuild.

## Cost & partitioning notes
- **Partition = `hour` (DAY granularity), `require_partition_filter=true`, no clustering.** Confirmed
  from `bq show` (timePartitioning.field=hour, type=DAY) AND empirically: a 2-column slice scales
  **2.37 MB (1 day) → 16.27 MB (7 days) = ~6.9×**, linear with the `hour` range → `hour` is the
  partition. **Always bound `hour`; there is no cluster key to prune within a day.**
- **Column-set cost (dry-run, 1-day slice 2026-04-10):**
  - `{advertiser_id, impressions}` (2 narrow cols): **2.37 MB/day** (16.27 MB for 7 days).
  - `SELECT *` (all 66 cols): **244.7 MB/day** — **~103× the 2-column slice.** The wide BYTES HLL
    sketches dominate; project only the measures you need.
  - HLL-merge over ~12 mixed cols (ids + spend + 2 sketches), 1 day: **0.099 GB billed** actual.
- **Backing storage** (physical `numBytes`, 2026-07-29): **~217 GB** (171 GB long-term), 66.15M rows,
  941 dated daily partitions (2024-01-01 → 2026-07-29) + 1 empty `__NULL__`. No table-level TTL/expiration.

## Example queries
```sql
-- Hourly delivery + revenue-side spend for one advertiser, one day (project narrow columns)
SELECT hour, campaign_group_id, impressions, video_impressions, display_impressions,
       media_spend, data_spend, platform_spend, media_cost
FROM `summarydata.sum_by_campaign_group_by_hour`
WHERE hour >= TIMESTAMP('2026-04-13') AND hour < TIMESTAMP('2026-04-14')
  AND advertiser_id = 31357;

-- UI "Verified Visits" (industry_standard) + reach for a campaign group over a week
SELECT campaign_group_id,
       SUM(clicks + views + competing_views)          AS verified_visits,
       HLL_COUNT.MERGE(uniques)                        AS households_reached
FROM `summarydata.sum_by_campaign_group_by_hour`
WHERE hour >= TIMESTAMP('2026-04-07') AND hour < TIMESTAMP('2026-04-14')
GROUP BY campaign_group_id;
```
